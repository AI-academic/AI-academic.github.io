Every issue is published here first. There are three ways to be told when a new
one appears, and none of them cost anything.

## By email

Join the mailing list: **[GROUP-NAME](https://groups.google.com/g/GROUP-NAME)**,
or send a blank email to
[GROUP-NAME+subscribe@googlegroups.com](mailto:GROUP-NAME+subscribe@googlegroups.com).

One message per issue, carrying the headline of every story in it and a link to
the full text. Nothing else is ever sent to the list, and you can leave at any
time using the link at the foot of each message.

## In a feed reader

The feed is at **[/feed.xml]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }})** and carries each
issue in full, so the whole briefing arrives in your reader without a click
through to this site. Any reader will take it — Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire,
Thunderbird.

## By email, without joining the list

If you would rather not be on a list, a feed-to-email service will mail you the
feed directly and let you choose the rhythm — as it appears, or a daily digest.
[Blogtrottr](https://blogtrottr.com/) is the straightforward one: give it the
feed address above and your own, and it does the rest.
