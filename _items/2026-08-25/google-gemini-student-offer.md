Google announced one-year student offers for its paid AI plans - Google AI Pro for eligible U.S. college students and Google AI Plus for eligible students in selected markets outside the United States. The offer includes higher Gemini limits and student-oriented features such as study notebooks, quizzes, deadline extraction from syllabi, interactive visualizations, and asynchronous Deep Research. The terms require eligibility verification and, for some offers, a payment method; plans may convert to paid subscriptions if not cancelled.
