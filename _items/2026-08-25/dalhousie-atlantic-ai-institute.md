Dalhousie announced the Senate-approved Atlantic AI Institute (A2I2), succeeding and broadening work begun through its Institute for Big Data Analytics. The institute launches with about 50 core members across 10 faculties and units, with more than 100 affiliated students and postdoctoral researchers. Its three pillars are research, academics, and community; planned activities include academic programs, microcredentials, mentorship, partnerships, and commercialization support.
