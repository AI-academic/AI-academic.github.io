Delaware State University announced its participation in the five-year, US$21.5-million NSF AI Institute for Human-AI Cooperation, led by the University of Delaware with the University of Pennsylvania, Princeton University, and the University of Florida. Delaware State will contribute research and infrastructure, lead engagement with 13 historically Black colleges and universities, and help develop internships and summer AI camps focused on health care and rehabilitation.
