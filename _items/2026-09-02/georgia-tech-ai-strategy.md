Georgia Tech released an Academic AI Strategy, which is organized around three priorities: AI fluency for every student, innovation across the academic enterprise, and institutional leadership and engagement. The accompanying playbook contains 67 faculty-authored articles from every college and distils 20 lessons from classroom experience. The institution frames disciplinary judgment and academic freedom as the basis for deciding how AI should and should not be used in teaching.
