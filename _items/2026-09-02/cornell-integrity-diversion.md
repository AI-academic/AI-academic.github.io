Cornell expanded its [Accepting Responsibility Program](https://deanoffaculty.cornell.edu/faculty-and-academic-affairs/academic-integrity/upcoming-code-of-academic-integrity/#accepting) campus-wide, which lets students with minor academic-integrity violations attend a workshop and write a reflection instead of facing a formal hearing. The two-year pilot covered roughly 360 students across 100 courses with four repeat violations. About half of participants in the pilot had used AI in violation of integrity standards; director Elizabeth Karns reported students saying they do not know how to do assignments without it.
