The U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences has awarded the University of California, Irvine US$10 million for the WRITE AI Center. The centre will survey GenAI use in postsecondary writing, publish information on widely used tools, develop and pilot the PapyrusAI writing environment with six community colleges, and conduct a randomized trial involving 60 instructors. Planned measures include writing quality, AI literacy, self-efficacy, course performance and persistence, as well as implementation cost, privacy, bias and fairness.
