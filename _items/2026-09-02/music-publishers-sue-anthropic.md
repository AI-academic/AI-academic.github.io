A set of music publishers that include Sony Music, Warner Chappell and others have sued Anthropic and co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann in the US District Court for the Northern District of California. They allege torrenting, scraping and downloading of copyrighted works including millions of copies of books containing lyrics and sheet music. The suit follows the Bartz litigation, where a judge held that training on copyrighted material could be lawful but acquiring it through piracy was not.
