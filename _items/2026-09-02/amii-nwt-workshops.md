Amii (https://www.amii.ca/) partnered with Prosper Northwest Territories (https://prospernwt.ca/) to run an AI workshop for seven Yellowknife businesses, supported by CanNor funding under $2.8 million federal initiative using a risk/value matrix to identify AI opportunities. The aim is to increase AI adoption in regions with limited access to technical expertise.
