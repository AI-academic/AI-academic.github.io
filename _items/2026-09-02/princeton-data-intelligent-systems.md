Princeton established Data and Intelligent Systems, a new academic unit combining Princeton AI with Princeton Statistics and Data Science. The Unit will coordinate research services, interdisciplinary projects, undergraduate and graduate education, and existing initiatives spanning AI-enabled invention, natural and artificial minds, and language and intelligence. Two new initiatives on AI alignment and safety and societal AI will launch this fall.
