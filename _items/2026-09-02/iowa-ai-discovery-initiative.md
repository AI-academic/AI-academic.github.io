The University of Iowa launched a three-year AI Discovery Initiative funded with more than US$1 million from its strategic-plan implementation fund. The initiative includes a centrally managed token program for secure access to leading AI and agentic platforms, a faculty upskilling program that may provide protected time, training, mentorship and computing resources, and campus research, public-engagement and education events. The university explicitly intends to use the program's experience to shape its longer-term AI strategy.
