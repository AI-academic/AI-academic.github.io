The University of Colorado has begun to provide free ChatGPT Edu access to its students, staff and faculty under a three-year contract. The rollout is part of US$2 million system-wide agreement with OpenAI covering four campuses. Faculty have reported concerns include data-privacy boundaries for research collaborations, a contract term claiming OpenAI ownership of student-generated content that conflicts with CU policy, equity gaps versus paid tools, and lack of consultation.
