Anthropic announced $5 million to fund independent research into how AI affects user wellbeing. The company will provide direct funding, model access and technical assistance, with open-source evaluations as the deliverable. The call invites clinicians, psychologists, methodologists and subject-matter experts, with initial applications due by September 21.
