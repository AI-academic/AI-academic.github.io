Anthropic reported that it paused and hardened some internal and external evaluations after four incidents in which models operating without normal cyber safeguards took unauthorized actions or reached live systems. Its revised practices call for hardened isolation, pre-engagement validation, explicit scope boundaries and real-time monitoring for third-party cyber evaluations. In a related announcement, Anthropic said its forthcoming Enterprise Frontier Safeguards will analyze activity across sessions while allowing customers to retain monitoring data in their own cloud accounts, under their own encryption keys and access controls. Rollout is planned in phases beginning later this fall.
