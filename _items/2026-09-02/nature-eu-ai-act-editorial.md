The editorial reports that the European AI Office gained enforcement powers on 2 August 2026, allowing investigations and sanctions up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover under the 2024 AI Act. The editorial endorses the Act's four-tier risk categorisation, calls for mandatory federal AI vetting in the US in place of the current voluntary 30-day review, and urges expanded AI Office staffing and researcher use of its whistle-blower mechanism.
