In a commentary published in the journal Nature, Robert Braun argues that conventional authorship cannot show where generative AI shaped literature search, analysis, drafting, revision or editorial work, even though human authors remain responsible for scientific claims. The author proposes a RediT-AI approach to document where AI influenced human contributions while keeping responsibility with researchers. The article itself discloses substantial use of ChatGPT in its development and Claude in review, making the piece a practical test of the transparency model it advocates.
