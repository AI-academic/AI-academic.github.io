A news feature examines AI-detection software for identifying machine-generated text in academic and research contexts, and assesses tools including Pangram and GPTZero and their use in peer review, student work and scientific publishing. The article reports that despite large accuracy gains, the tools still struggle to separate fully AI-generated text from human writing lightly polished with AI — the most common real-world case.
