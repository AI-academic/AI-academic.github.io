Berkeley's College of Computing, Data Science, and Society announced a Master of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, a full-time in-person degree launching fall 2027. Coursework spans data science, machine learning, probability, statistical inference and AI ethics, plus a Silicon Valley immersion and a comprehensive examination.
