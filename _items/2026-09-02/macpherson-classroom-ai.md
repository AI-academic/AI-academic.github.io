Dr. Felicia Vulcu (Biochemistry and Biomedical Sciences) documented three practices in her laboratory course: an optional AI-use policy specifying which assessments permit generative AI, requiring students to verify accuracy and disclose use; using ChatGPT to generate deliberately flawed articles for students to critique and rewrite; and generating scientific illustrations and a gel-electrophoresis simulator to prepare students before lab work. She reports students adhered to the policy on take-home assignments and plans to survey students and TAs.
