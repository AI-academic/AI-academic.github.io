A Science and Technology Australia survey of more than 1,000 researchers, reported by Times Higher Education, found that this year applicants sought over 2.2 billion compute hours, nearly three times the combined capacity of Australia's two national facilities, the National Computational Infrastructure (Canberra) and the Pawsey Supercomputing Research Centre (Perth). Machine learning is driving the growth, and over 95% of estimated demand for both CPU and GPU capacity came from just ten respondents. The survey result shows that discounted commercial compute is not an easy substitute because only 10% of respondents said they would use it, and a further 36% would consider it if barriers of access, cost and technical support were removed.
