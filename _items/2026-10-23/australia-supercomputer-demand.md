A Science and Technology Australia survey of more than 1,000 researchers, reported by Times Higher Education, found that applicants this year sought over 2.2 billion compute hours, nearly three times the combined capacity of Australia's two national facilities, the National Computational Infrastructure in Canberra and the Pawsey Supercomputing Research Centre in Perth. Machine learning is driving the growth, and over 95% of estimated demand for both CPU and GPU capacity came from just ten respondents. Discounted commercial compute is not an easy substitute: only 10% of respondents said they would use it, and a further 36% would consider it if barriers of access, cost and technical support were removed.
