Italy's decree bringing its law in line with the EU AI Act took effect on 23 October. It names the national bodies that will oversee AI and includes a section on AI use and training in schools, universities, government and the workplace. Secondary-school and university students are to be taught how to use AI responsibly and what its risks are, and training also extends to teachers, lecturers, parents and adult learners. The decree sets aside up to €100 million to address the educational challenges of social media and AI, and gives the government until about 21 January 2027 to set criteria for teacher training. It also sets up a supervised space for testing AI systems and calls for joint research projects with universities.
