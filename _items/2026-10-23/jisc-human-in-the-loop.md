Jisc's National Centre for AI in Tertiary Education is recruiting UK colleges and universities for the discovery phase of its Human in the Loop Project. Each participating institution picks one bounded AI-supported process, then defines, designs and peer-reviews an oversight approach for it using AI tools it already holds. The discovery phase runs from late November 2026 to mid-February 2027, and joining does not commit an institution to the later implementation phase; sign-ups close 27 November 2026. Participating institutions will be named unless they ask not to be, but findings will not be attributed to them. The project starts from a pointed question: whether a person who merely approves an AI output is meaningfully "in the loop" at all.
