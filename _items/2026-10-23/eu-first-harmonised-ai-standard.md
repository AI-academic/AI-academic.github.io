The European Commission has confirmed the first official standard supporting the EU AI Act (EN 18286:2026). The standard sets out the quality-management system that providers of high-risk AI systems must have under the Act, and is designed to sit on top of ISO/IEC 42001, the main international standard for managing AI. Providers that follow it are presumed to meet those parts of the law. The standard was formally recognized in the EU’s Official Journal on October 14, and the Commission announced it on October 19. The high-risk rules for most standalone systems apply from December 2027, including AI use in education, and for AI built into regulated products from August 2028.
