The European Commission has confirmed the first official standard supporting the EU AI Act, EN 18286:2026. It sets out the quality-management system that providers of high-risk AI systems must have under the Act, and is designed to sit on top of ISO/IEC 42001, the main international standard for managing AI. Providers that follow it are presumed to meet those parts of the law. The standard was formally recognised in the EU's Official Journal on 14 October, and the Commission announced it on 19 October. The high-risk rules apply from December 2027 for most standalone systems, including AI used in education, and from August 2028 for AI built into regulated products.
