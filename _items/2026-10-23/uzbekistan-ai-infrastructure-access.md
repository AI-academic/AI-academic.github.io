Uzbekistan has approved free or discounted access to the Ministry of Digital Technologies' AI infrastructure for socially significant projects run by universities, scientific organizations, startups and regional governors' advisers on AI. Selected large-language-model projects can receive up to 10 million tokens a month free for six months, with a 50% discount on additional use; other AI model projects can apply for up to 120 GB of GPU memory for training, testing and running models. Applications are judged in competitions held twice a year, in July and November.
