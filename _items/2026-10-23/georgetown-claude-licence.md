Georgetown's chief information officer, Douglas Little, told the Faculty Senate on 19 October that the university is finalizing a licence for Anthropic's Claude. It would follow a Gemini rollout to faculty and staff in March and an opt-in ChatGPT premium pilot for faculty and staff in October. The university is still negotiating protections and terms, and expects to sign by the end of the fall semester and extend licences to some faculty by then. Officials did not say whether students will get access, and no cost was reported. The report is from the student newspaper, and the contract is not yet signed.
