Georgetown university is finalizing a license for Anthropic's Claude. It would follow a Gemini rollout to faculty and staff in March and an opt-in ChatGPT premium pilot for faculty and staff in October. The university is still negotiating protections and terms, and expects to sign by the end of fall 2026 and extend licenses to some faculty by then. Officials did not say whether students will get access, and no cost was reported. The report is from the student newspaper, and the contract is not yet signed.
