In a Nature opinion column, Fields medallist Terence Tao argues that AI now produces proofs faster than mathematicians can understand them. He says this is already hurting the field. Results are poorly explained, researchers fear being scooped, and junior mathematicians are losing the problems they once trained on. He wants mathematics to reward understanding over being first, and to build its own open-source AI tools rather than rely on AI companies.
