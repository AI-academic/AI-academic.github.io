The University of Waterloo's Global Futures Office approved more than $1 million in Scale and Leverage grants for three Global Futures Initiatives, two of which are AI-centred. Scaling Waterloo.AI organizes the university's AI researchers into four research clusters, linked by a bridge program that funds micro-internships and fellowships. Can-View is building a national eye-data repository for AI-driven research, policy and innovation in vision care.
