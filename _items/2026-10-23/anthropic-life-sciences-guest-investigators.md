Anthropic invited life-sciences researchers to apply for 8 - 12 week collaborations working directly with Anthropic researchers to produce analyses, tools and other outputs. Principal investigators and their groups at academic institutions, nonprofits and government labs are eligible, and the work covers areas from human biology and molecular biology to medicinal chemistry, drug discovery and clinical science. Anthropic will run its models at no cost to participants, results will be co-published with data and code open-sourced, and investigators keep the rights to their data. Applications are open for about two weeks, with no closing date stated, and selected participants are notified about two weeks after the window closes.
