The Ontario Undergraduate Student Alliance has published a policy paper on AI, written by undergraduate students and ratified by student representatives from its member institutions in March 2026. It identifies concerns involving accessibility, academic integrity, privacy and environmental effects, and describes gaps in provincial legislation, institutional oversight, AI literacy and research on student impacts. Its recommendations include requiring every institution to adopt a standalone AI policy developed with students and staff, setting AI rules specific to postsecondary education in provincial law, and ending the use of AI-detection tools in academic integrity investigations.
