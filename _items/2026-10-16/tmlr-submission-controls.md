The editors of Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR) reported 300% submission growth over the past year and a 1,300% increase in solo-authored papers. The journal has introduced co-authorship-weighted submission quotas, one-year review suspensions for policy violations or multiple very low-quality submissions, public identification of well-supported major integrity violations, and an AI-generated assessment of each submission's soundness to assist human editors. Its desk-rejection rate has risen from 6% in 2023 to about 53%. The editors do not say how much of the growth is driven by generative AI.
