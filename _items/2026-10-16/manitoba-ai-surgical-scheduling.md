A University of Manitoba professor and orthopedic surgeon, Dr. Jay Toor, leads a project funded by a Research Manitoba Solutions Grant that uses AI and large language models to forecast surgical needs and improve scheduling. The work builds on ORION, an AI scheduling system first piloted at Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre in Toronto. It is projected that orthopedic surgical capacity will increase by 5 - 8%, along with 10 - 15% lower wait times and $2 - 4 million in annual savings.
