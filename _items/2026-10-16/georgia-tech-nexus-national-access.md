Georgia Tech has opened Nexus, an AI supercomputer built with US$20 million in National Science Foundation funding in partnership with the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, to the US research community. At least 90% of its capacity goes to researchers outside Georgia Tech through an open, peer-reviewed allocation process, and the system will support the National AI Research Resource. Allocation by peer review rather than by host-institution priority makes it a concrete model for a university-hosted AI system run as a national shared resource.
