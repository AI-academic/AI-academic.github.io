University of Toronto President Melanie Woodin set out three near-term commitments for the university: 1) preparing future-ready graduates, 2) advancing responsible AI adoption at scale, and 3) mobilizing knowledge for social and economic impact. She described U of T's three campuses as a "living laboratory" for responsible AI adoption. Current implementation includes a forthcoming enterprise-wide AI platform using Cohere's North as an orchestration layer, and computing infrastructure that recently received $42.5 million from the federal Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy. Under the third commitment, U of T is partnering with McMaster University, Venture Ontario, and Genesys Capital on a life-sciences venture fund. A broader consultative process on the university's long-term priorities is set to begin in 2027.
