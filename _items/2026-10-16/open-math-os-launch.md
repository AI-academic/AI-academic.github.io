The SAIR Foundation has soft-launched Open Math OS, a platform offering academic mathematicians free compute on existing open-weight models, currently Kimi K3, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash. The platform aims to reach 10,000 mathematicians within three months, beginning with 1,000 research teams by November 2026. The default for projects is private access and users will retain ownership of their work. Training on user work requires consent. The models are roughly a year behind the frontier. The platform is not built for one-shot problem solving but useful for medium-sized Lean formalization. Team members can see one another's prompts. Unlike OpenAI's Rosalind plan, this offer is not limited by country, and its published data terms serve as a useful benchmark to evaluate other vendor academic agreements.
