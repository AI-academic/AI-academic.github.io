The SAIR Foundation has soft-launched Open Math OS, a platform offering academic mathematicians free compute on existing open-weight models, currently Kimi K3, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash. It aims to reach 10,000 mathematicians within three months, beginning with 1,000 research teams by November 2026. Projects are private by default, training on user work requires consent, and users retain ownership. Terence Tao has described the models as roughly a year behind the frontier, not built for one-shot problem solving but useful for medium-sized Lean formalization; team members can see one another's prompts. Unlike OpenAI's Rosalind plan, this offer is not limited by country, and its published data terms are a useful benchmark against which to read vendor academic agreements.
