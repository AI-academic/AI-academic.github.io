The federal government has added C$500 million over five years to the Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative, bringing total program funding to $700 million. Delivered by the seven regional development agencies under the national 'AI for All' strategy, the program supports responsible AI adoption by businesses and organizations, commercialization and scale-up of Canadian AI products, and regional ecosystem capacity. Priority sectors include health and life sciences, energy and natural resources, transportation, agriculture, manufacturing, and robotics.
