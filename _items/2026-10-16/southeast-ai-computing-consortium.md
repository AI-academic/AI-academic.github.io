Fifteen universities in ten states, including Alabama, Texas A&M, Vanderbilt and UT Austin, have formed the Southeast Region Super-Intelligence Computing Consortium, a public-private partnership to pool AI computing capacity. The stated long-term goal is to develop and operate one or more leadership-class AI computing systems in the region. No industry partners were named in the announcement.
