Rice University launched an interdisciplinary initiative to hire AI-focused faculty in foundational AI, AI for health, AI for energy and resilience, and responsible AI; it has recruited 35 such faculty so far and plans 40 more this decade. Rice is also planning an AI Foundry within The Arc, a roughly 200,000-square-foot research and innovation hub, and will introduce a Master of Artificial Intelligence in fall 2027. The university has not disclosed a total financial commitment for the initiative.
