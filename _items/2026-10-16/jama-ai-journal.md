JAMA Network announced JAMA AI as its fourteenth journal, opening to submissions in early 2027 and covering clinical research on generative and agentic AI, decision support and digital health. The existing JAMA+ AI channel continues. A new venue provides a place where health-AI work is expected to be found, and it matters for health-sciences researchers planning submissions and for how AI work is weighted in evaluation.
