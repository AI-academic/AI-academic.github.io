OpenAI launched the Rosalind Academic Plan for life-sciences research. Approved principal investigators or lab leads at eligible US universities can receive up to 30 ChatGPT Business Standard seats at no cost for up to 12 months. The access to the specialized GPT-Rosalind model is subject to institutional verification and trusted-access review. OpenAI says plan data is not used for model training by default and it is limited to US universities for now. OpenAI says expansion to other countries is planned.
