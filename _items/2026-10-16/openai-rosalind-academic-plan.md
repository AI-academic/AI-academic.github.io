OpenAI launched the Rosalind Academic Plan for life-sciences research. Approved principal investigators or lab leads at eligible US universities can receive up to 30 ChatGPT Business Standard seats at no cost for up to 12 months, with access to the specialized GPT-Rosalind model subject to institutional verification and trusted-access review. Each application must name a trained biosafety contact designated by the institution. OpenAI says plan data is not used for model training by default; API access is not included. The plan is limited to US universities for now, though OpenAI says expansion to other countries is planned, and the biosafety requirement is notable as a vendor imposing an institutional governance precondition on research access.
