Georgia Tech and Emory University announced that their combined AI commitments exceed US$1 billion, including approximately US$600 million at Emory and more than US$575 million at Georgia Tech. The portfolio spans advanced computing, including the Nexus AI supercomputer and a campus-wide computing platform, as well as faculty recruitment, health applications, workforce development, and commercialization. The announcement describes each institution's own investments and programs rather than a single new joint fund.
