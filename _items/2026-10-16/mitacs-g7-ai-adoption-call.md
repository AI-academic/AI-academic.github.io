Mitacs has opened a "GRA G7 Artificial Intelligence (AI) Adoption Call" under its Globalink Research Award, funding research mobility between Canada and the other G7 countries (France, Germany, Italy, Japan, the United Kingdom and the United States). The call prioritizes AI research with a route to real-world use, especially projects that could support adoption by small and medium-sized enterprises. Senior undergraduates, graduate students and postdoctoral fellows at Mitacs full-member universities are eligible. There is no fixed deadline: applications are processed as received until the funding is used up.
