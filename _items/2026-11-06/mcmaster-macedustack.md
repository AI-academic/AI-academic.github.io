Fazmin Nizam, IT Infrastructure and AI Lead in McMaster's Faculty of Engineering, has described MacEduStack, a web workspace where AI drafts assessments, rubrics and multiple-choice questions from course materials, and instructors review everything before students see it. Students also get practice quizzes and a reflection journal. Generative AI does not make grading decisions: quizzes are marked against an answer key. The tool is still a work in progress and not connected to the university's learning management system; the MacPherson Institute is setting up a pilot study, and wider use awaits retention schedules, agreements with AI providers and a privacy impact assessment.
