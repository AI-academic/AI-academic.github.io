The IT Infrastructure and AI Lead in McMaster's Faculty of Engineering, Fazmin Nizam, has developed MacEduStack, a web workspace where AI drafts assessments, rubrics, and multiple-choice questions from course materials. The instructors review everything before students see it. Students also get practice quizzes and a reflection journal. Generative AI does not make grading decisions: quizzes are marked against an answer key. The tool is a work in progress and not yet implemented at the university. The MacPherson Institute is setting up a pilot study, and wider use awaits retention schedules, agreements with AI providers, and a privacy impact assessment.
