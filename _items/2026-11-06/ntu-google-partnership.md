Nanyang Technological University Singapore and Google announced a memorandum of understanding on 30 October covering campus-wide AI integration, Southeast Asian language and sovereign-model development with AI Singapore, frontier research and AI safety. NTU aims to embed AI in 40 per cent of undergraduate courses by 2030, will explore linking Gemini Enterprise to its NTU Learn platform, and will offer faculty Google-accredited training. The partners also plan open regional-language datasets, education and health models built on Google's open-weight Gemma models, and a secure research sandbox that NTU will build on Google Cloud. On 4 November NTU announced a separate US$750,000 award from Google.org's AI for Science Capacity Fund, initially aimed at PhD education and research.
