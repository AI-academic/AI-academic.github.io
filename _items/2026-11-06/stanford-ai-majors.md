Stanford University has launched a US$10-million, provost-funded initiative to help departments redesign whole undergraduate majors for the AI era, from introductory courses to capstones, rather than isolated courses. Beginning in winter quarter 2027, departments and programs can apply on a rolling, quarterly basis for one- to two-year grants expected to range from US$250,000 to US$750,000. Initiative staff will help them map AI-related learning outcomes across courses, pathways and sequences, including different stances toward the technology. Stanford expects to work with about 15 departments, concluding by 2028-29.
