Vice-Chancellor P. Raveendran told an expert committee meeting in Kozhikode on 5 November that the University of Calicut will overhaul its undergraduate and postgraduate curricula to incorporate AI and foundational computing, using existing infrastructure and without adding to teachers' workload. The changes still need Syndicate and Academic Council approval, and a committee led by K. George Thomas is guiding the revision under state and national academic frameworks. MOOCs will be offered in Malayalam and English, and a repository of new courses due in December is meant to let affiliated colleges choose programmes rather than follow a single template.
