Vice-Chancellor of the University of Calicut announced that the university will overhaul its undergraduate and postgraduate curricula to incorporate AI and foundational computing, using existing infrastructure and without adding to teachers' workload. The changes still need Syndicate and Academic Council approval, and a committee is guiding the revision under state and national academic frameworks. MOOCs will be offered in a regional language (Malayalam) and English, and a repository of new courses due in December is meant to let affiliated colleges choose programmes rather than follow a single template.
