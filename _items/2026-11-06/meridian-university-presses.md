Cambridge, Johns Hopkins, and Princeton university presses are the founding publishers of Meridian, an initiative convened by AI data-infrastructure company Cashmere. It offers a single gateway through which AI tools can retrieve authorized, cited scholarly content, with usage reporting for libraries, and content reached through it is never used to train or fine-tune AI models. Founding publishers begin onboarding their catalogues in November, with a closed pilot for selected institutions and libraries later in the fall. Cashmere has committed its platform for an initial 24-month evaluation and intends Meridian to become an independent, community-governed not-for-profit once it can sustain itself.
