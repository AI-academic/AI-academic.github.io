A *Nature Geoscience* editorial sets out Springer Nature's risk-assessment framework for AI in publishing, which sorts uses by authors, editors and reviewers into permitted, requires-caution and prohibited categories and requires disclosure of AI use across a publication's life cycle. Authors remain fully responsible for their work, AI tools used for data analysis must be disclosed, and manuscripts and other non-public material should not be shared with open or unsecured AI systems. The editorial states that AI "should not replace human decision making" in editorial and review workflows, and that the policies will keep evolving.
