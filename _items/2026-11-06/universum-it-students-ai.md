A survey carried out by a Swedish research and advisory company, Universum, found that about 65% of Canadian IT students were confident about their careers after graduation, against 84% in engineering and 79% in business. The survey included roughly 100 thousand students from business, engineering, and IT fields across nine countries, of which 3.2% were from Canada.

Among the IT students, 81% said they are developing AI skills and only 3% said they do not use AI at all, yet the share positive or optimistic about working for an employer that uses AI fell to 63% from 73% in 2025. The article notes that the survey did not directly link AI use to career confidence.
