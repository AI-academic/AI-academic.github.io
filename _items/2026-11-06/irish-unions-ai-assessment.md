The Teachers' Union of Ireland (TUI) and the Irish Federation of University Teachers (IFUT) set out a joint position that relying on AI-detection tools or banning AI is no longer enough, and that assessment must be rethought. Both unions oppose a ban. They want more investment in staff training and resources, clear AI guidelines that are regularly updated, and staff representation in decisions about how AI is used in higher education. IFUT general secretary Frank Jones described "a seismic and fundamental shift" in how students and academics engage with teaching, learning and assessment, and TUI general secretary Michael Gillespie called it an "era-defining challenge."
