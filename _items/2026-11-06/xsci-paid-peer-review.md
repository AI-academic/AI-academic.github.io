*Nature* reports on XSci, a preprint platform launched in September 2026 that pays human reviewers US$200 for feedback delivered within five days of accepting, or $100 within five to ten days, funded by authors who pay $399 per review round ($249 if the report is made public). XSci also charges authors up to $19 for reviews from a range of AI programs, with some AI tools free, and uses its own AI program to rank candidate reviewers and email them one at a time. Reviewers are drawn from authors the paper cites, their co-authors and people who publish in the target journals. The scheme has reignited debate over whether peer reviewers should be paid.
