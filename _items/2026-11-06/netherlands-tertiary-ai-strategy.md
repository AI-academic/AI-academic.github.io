The Dutch House of Representatives have asked the government for an AI strategy covering vocational (MBO), higher professional (HBO) and university (WO) education. The strategy would focus on AI literacy, including critical thinking, source research, recognizing disinformation and misinformation, and understanding AI's limitations. Universities of the Netherlands backed it, saying students should be "well prepared for a future in which AI plays an increasingly significant role."
