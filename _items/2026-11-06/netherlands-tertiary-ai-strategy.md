A majority of the Dutch House of Representatives backed a motion by D66 MP Ilana Rooderkerk asking the government for an AI strategy covering vocational (MBO), higher professional (HBO) and university (WO) education. The strategy would focus on AI literacy, including critical thinking, source research, recognising disinformation and misinformation, and understanding AI's limitations. Only Forum for Democracy and the PVV did not support the motion. Universities of the Netherlands backed it, saying students should be "well prepared for a future in which AI plays an increasingly significant role."
