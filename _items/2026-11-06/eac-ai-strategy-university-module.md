At the 21st Sectoral Council on Education, Science and Technology, Culture and Sports, held 27–31 October in Kampala, East African Community ministers adopted the EAC Artificial Intelligence Strategy alongside digital-transformation and biotechnology strategies. They approved a foundational AI module for university students and directed partner states to consider integrating it into degree programmes, with the Inter-University Council for East Africa (IUCEA) to develop a regional implementation framework. The Council also approved a Regional Artificial Intelligence Network of Universities, three AI instruments for teacher education, and AI certification benchmarks for Kiswahili. Officials warned that limited funding could slow implementation, and acknowledged Germany, the EU, and the World Bank for supporting the EAC AI Alliance.
