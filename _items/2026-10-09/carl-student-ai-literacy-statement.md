The Canadian Association of Research Libraries (CARL) has released a statement on the role of academic libraries in advancing student AI literacy. It argues that libraries are the natural campus leads for this work, able to address academic integrity, privacy, bias, authorship and responsible AI use by building on their existing information, digital, and copyright literacy programs. The statement calls for critical evaluation of AI, including how systems are trained and how they use personal data, to be treated as a foundational skill for every student. It does not say how this role would fit with the work of faculty and departmental leads or other teaching support units.
