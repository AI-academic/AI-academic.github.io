The Government of Canada issued a Security Policy Implementation Notice, effective October 1, requiring federal organizations to apply baseline cyber security controls, identify and fix material gaps that AI-enabled threat actors could exploit, and operate on an assume-breach basis. Its scope explicitly includes high-performance computing, cloud and on-premises systems, network- or Wi-Fi-connected laboratory equipment and every other IP-addressable networked asset. Departments must submit a prioritized list of critical services by November 30. Although the direction applies to federal organizations rather than universities, its control perimeter is a relevant public-sector comparator for research environments.
