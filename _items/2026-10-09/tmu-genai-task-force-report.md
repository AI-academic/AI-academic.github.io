Toronto Metropolitan University's Leadership Task Force on Generative AI, launched in October 2025, published its report on October 1. Its recommendations are organised around five areas: 1) policy, guidelines and governance; 2) technological infrastructure; 3) training and professional development; 4) learning, teaching and student success; and 5) scholarly, research and creative activity. The central structural choice is to adapt and strengthen TMU's existing policies to account for AI rather than create a standalone AI policy, under three guiding principles: equitable access and inclusion, ethical and responsible use, and compliance with data privacy and security obligations. The university says it will keep assessing and critiquing AI's effects on society as it applies the technology, prioritizing uses that amplify human intellect and agency over transactional efficiency.
