Several UK universities are leaving Turnitin or reviewing their use of it. Newcastle will stop after the 2026-27 academic year, citing the potential use of staff and student data for AI purposes, and Westminster will retire it when its contract ends in July 2027, calling Turnitin's AI detection probabilistic rather than conclusive; Southampton had already announced its exit, and Aberdeen is reviewing its use. Cambridge says it will not accept licence changes that affect data privacy or intellectual property. After objections raised through the UK sector body UCISA, Turnitin postponed the licence changes to September 2027, and it says it trains only a classifier that flags likely AI-written text, not a generative model.
