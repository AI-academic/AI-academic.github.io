The UK government accepted all 44 recommendations of the National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare and committed to a lifecycle-based framework in which AI-enabled medical devices are assessed and monitored throughout their working life. A roadmap assigning timelines and responsibilities for every recommendation is due by spring 2027. The Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) has opened a third phase of its AI Airlock sandbox focused on post-market surveillance and will publish draft guidance on managing changes to AI-enabled devices by December 2026.
