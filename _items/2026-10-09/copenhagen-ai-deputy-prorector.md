The University of Copenhagen has appointed anthropologist Morten Axel Pedersen to a newly created post of deputy prorector for AI. The university has committed DKr110 million (about £13 million) to an AI office, a cross-university task force, AI labs, an in-house generative AI assistant, and an "AI traffic light" that shows staff which data may be used with which systems. Over the next year, Pedersen will review every course to decide whether AI should be included, excluded or required, using a temporary "scaffold" that lets each discipline set its own relationship with AI. He describes current staff and student use as taking place "in the shadows."
