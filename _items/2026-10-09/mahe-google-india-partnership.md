The Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) signed a memorandum of understanding with Google India under MAGIC, its MAHE Agentic Intelligence Console initiative, which aims to make MAHE a fully agentic-AI university. The parties plan to work toward a Google-powered AI Centre of Excellence for healthcare research using Gemini and other Google tools, strengthen AI-enabled teaching and learning, and give faculty and students access to new AI tools. MAHE had already partnered with OpenAI in May on AI across teaching, research and administration.
