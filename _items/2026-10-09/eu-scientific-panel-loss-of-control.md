The European Commission convened a special meeting of the Scientific Panel on AI to discuss recent loss-of-control incidents involving frontier models. The panel of independent experts advises the AI Office and national authorities on systemic risk. With the AI Office, it has prepared a set of questions for the companies that developed the models involved. The Commission did not name the incidents, models or companies.
