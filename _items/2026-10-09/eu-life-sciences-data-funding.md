The European Commission amended the Horizon Europe work programme to propose €65 million for life-sciences data and knowledge resources that underpin new medicines and AI tools for biotechnology. Subject to grant evaluation, the package assigns €19 million to GENCODE, €30 million to UniProt and €8 million to Europe PMC, plus an €8-million open call for other repositories and services. The resources are to remain openly accessible to researchers worldwide.
