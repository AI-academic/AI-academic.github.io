OpenAI says the EU AI Act requires generative AI providers to make generated text identifiable in a machine-readable way. Over the coming weeks it will add an invisible watermark to eligible ChatGPT and Codex output in the EU, but not as a global default, and watermarking stays opt-in for API customers. The detector will be open only to approved researchers and expert organizations on a case by case basis, partly because of the risk of missed watermarks and false positives. OpenAI said that a missing watermark does not prove human authorship.
