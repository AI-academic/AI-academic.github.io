Georgia Tech and Amazon launched a joint science hub supporting research projects between Georgia Tech faculty and Amazon scientists, along with symposia, workshops and community programs. Initial work will address autonomous systems, next-generation logistics infrastructure and low-Earth-orbit communications. Georgia Tech is also joining Fauna Robotics' ecosystem to use its Sprout platform for research on mobility, perception, manipulation and human-robot interaction. Amazon will fund joint research and community-building, but the announcement states no value or term.
