On October 6, OpenAI posted 722 manuscripts produced by an internal frontier model to GitHub, claiming progress on 372 mathematics problems. A day later it withdrew three because of a sign error and revised 14 others. OpenAI says it drew on advice from the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study, whose guidelines for AI labs had earlier asked them to stop testing advanced mathematical problems on proprietary models. The group says it is up to mathematicians to judge how far those guidelines were followed. The Association for Human Mathematics urged colleagues to stop working with OpenAI.
