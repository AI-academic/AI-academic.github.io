The State University of New York (SUNY) and Google Public Sector launched a secure research environment that combines storage, high-performance computing, Gemini Enterprise and more than 200 AI models. It will initially serve nine of SUNY's most research-intensive campuses on a common foundation with consistent governance. SUNY is also offering Google's self-paced AI credentials system-wide at no cost to students and faculty, and is working to embed Google Career Certificates in academic programs.
