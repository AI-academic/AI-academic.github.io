The article by Dan Sarofian-Butin (Merrimack College) reports running the Pangram detector on 2025 doctoral dissertations in educational leadership: about 35% contained more than a negligible share of AI-generated text and about 10% were more than half AI-generated. The author cites a working paper reporting 30–40% AI-generated text in STEM PhD dissertations, and proposes the three-study and dissertation-in-practice formats as alternatives. The analysis is unpublished and not peer reviewed, and AI-detector reliability is contested.
