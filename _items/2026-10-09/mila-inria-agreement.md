Mila and Inria, France's national institute for research in digital science and technology, have agreed a four-year cooperation. The agreement covers foundational machine learning and agentic AI, AI evaluation and safety, AI for science and health, trustworthy AI, robotics, advanced computing infrastructure, energy and environmental sustainability, and the societal and economic impacts of AI. Planned activities include joint projects, co-supervision of master's and PhD students, and mobility for researchers and students, with applied priorities in quantum plus AI, open source and multilingual AI. A joint steering committee will run the agreement, and other Canadian and French organizations may join as new initiatives emerge. The open-membership clause should allow Canadian universities to join the agreement.
