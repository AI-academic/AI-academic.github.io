US President Donald Trump issued an executive order on September 29 declaring that today's systems represent "a new era of Super Intelligence," arguing their capabilities far exceed what was envisioned when the term "artificial intelligence" came into use. Federal agencies are directed, as far as the law permits, to use "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in place of "artificial intelligence" and "AI" in correspondence, websites, reports and policy documents; existing regulations, contracts and grants need not be changed. The order defines Super Intelligence as everything the statutory term "artificial intelligence" already covers, whereas researchers use superintelligence for hypothetical systems that exceed human ability across almost every domain, a gap Nature's October 7 explainer examines.
