MIT President Sally Kornbluth announced MIT for America, a national STEM initiative reaching learners from kindergarten to community college. Its AI pillar, "Living, learning, and working with AI," covers teacher education for bringing AI into classroom learning and programs that build AI skills at technical-vocational schools and community colleges. The other two pillars are mathematical thinking and problem solving, and designing, making and innovating.
