The European Commission published Friend or foe?, an education policy paper synthesizing emerging evidence on generative AI in teaching and learning. The higher-education evidence behind it finds that clearly communicated institutional and discipline-specific rules shape student use and reduce misuse, and that staff AI literacy and confidence are key to guiding students responsibly. The school-education evidence distinguishes engagement and AI-assisted task success from durable learning, and recommends some assessment without AI access. The paper identifies personalization and teacher-workload benefits alongside bias, privacy and overreliance risks.
