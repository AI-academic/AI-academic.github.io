CRANE, a cybersecurity funding pilot hosted at the University of Oxford and funded by EPSRC, part of UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), used AI to decide which of 179 proposals went on to human review. Almost half of applications were rejected without a person reading them. After academic criticism, CRANE apologized, agreed to re-review every application screened out by AI and to reconvene its funding panel, and suspended offers already made. CRANE said it used closed, ring-fenced models with no internet access rather than public services such as ChatGPT.
