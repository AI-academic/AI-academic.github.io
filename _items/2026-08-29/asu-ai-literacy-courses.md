Arizona State University (ASU) announced that, beginning in fall 2026, every college and school will offer an AI literacy course or pathway that counts toward a student's major. Faculty designed the offerings for their disciplines, and instructors retain authority to set assignment-level rules for AI use. ASU also reported an assessment-design initiative that focused on its 100 highest-enrolment courses, and included more than 400 AI workshops for faculty and staff and over 45,000 users of its institutionally supported CreateAI environment.
