OpenAI published an open letter supported by more than 100 technology, cybersecurity, finance and infrastructure organizations, including Anthropic, AWS, Google, Hugging Face, IBM and Microsoft. The signatories state that AI-enabled cyberattacks will become more widespread and sophisticated in the coming months. They call on organizations to fix high-risk weaknesses, strengthen access controls and apply a higher security bar to AI-generated code; on governments to fund and coordinate defence for under-resourced essential services; and on frontier AI companies to provide responsible model access, observability, traceable agent identities, training and support. The letter sets out voluntary principles but does not specify binding commitments, budgets or delivery deadlines.
