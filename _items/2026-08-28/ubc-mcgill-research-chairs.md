The University of British Columbia will recruit nine Eddie Goldenberg Research Chairs of Canada and McGill University will recruit six chairs with $52 million in federal support over eight years. UBC and McGill can also recruit nine and six Canada Impact+ Emerging Leaders, respectively. The appointments form part of the first national intake of 64 chairs and 63 emerging leaders supported by more than $541 million.
