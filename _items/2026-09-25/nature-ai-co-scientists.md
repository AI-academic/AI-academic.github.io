A Nature technology feature looks at the AI "co-scientist" systems now generating hypotheses, searching the literature and designing experiments across biomedical research, among them Google's Co-Scientist, FutureHouse's Kosmos and Stanford's Virtual Lab. It points to a training paradox that bears directly on graduate education: researchers need deeper expertise to judge what these systems propose, yet students get fewer chances to build that expertise through hands-on discovery. One of Virtual Lab's developers puts the limit simply: "If you ask very generic questions, then you'll probably get very generic answers." Deciding which problems are worth asking about is still human work.
