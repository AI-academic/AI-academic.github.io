A Nature news feature asks whether generative AI is weakening students' ability to reason for themselves. So far the research is mixed and does not yet show clearly what the technology is doing to learning, though some cognitive scientists expect a gradual decline that will take years to show. The piece argues that critical thinking is not a generic skill and it does not transfer from one field to another. It has to be taught within specific subjects, and it depends on effortful thinking.
