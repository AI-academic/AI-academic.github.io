The University of Calgary Students' Union made written and verbal submissions to a panel set up by Alberta's Ministry of Advanced Education to look at AI's effects on post-secondary students and graduates. The panel met on September 18 and is due to publish its findings by the end of 2026. The union argued that leaning on AI risks leaving students without the critical-thinking skills they need for the job market, and recommended that universities stop using AI for grading and go back to conventional assessment. It also asked for clearer rules on AI use, through institutional policy or provincial legislation, and for attention to data security. Union president Gabriela Dziegielewska said AI "should not be adopted just for adoption's sake," calling it a convenient tool "for somebody who already has the skills and knowledge to be able to evaluate the outputs."
