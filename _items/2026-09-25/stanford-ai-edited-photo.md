Stanford's Residential and Dining Enterprises used generative AI to rework a photo from a 2024 Lunar New Year dinner for a housing banner. The edit dressed students in Stanford merchandise, slimmed two students' faces, and replaced one student with someone of a different race and gender. A university spokesperson told the Stanford Daily that using AI to produce or alter images of Stanford people, events, research, facilities or achievements is not allowed, and that both the alteration and the failure to disclose it broke that policy. Stanford says all staff will get further training and that materials will be properly reviewed from now on.
