Syracuse University has launched an Institute for Artificial Intelligence that draws faculty from four schools and colleges. Its work is organized around four areas: AI and security, multidomain intelligence, AI in socio-cognitive dynamics, and AI for governance and policy. The institute sits alongside Syracuse's existing AI degrees, minors and AI bootcamp, and will be supported through collaboration and funding agreements with federal research agencies and industry partners.
