Syracuse University has launched an Institute for Artificial Intelligence that draws faculty from four schools and colleges and is led by Paulo Shakarian of the College of Engineering and Computer Science. Its work is organised around four areas: AI and security, multidomain intelligence, AI in socio-cognitive dynamics, and AI for governance and policy. The institute sits alongside Syracuse's existing AI degrees, minors and AI bootcamp, and will be supported through collaboration and funding agreements with federal research agencies and industry partners. The Army Research Office is sponsoring its first workshop this fall.
