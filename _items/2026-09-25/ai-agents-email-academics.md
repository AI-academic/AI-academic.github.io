Nature reports that semi-autonomous AI agents are emailing academics directly with unsolicited requests for collaboration, data, donations and paid work. An NYU philosopher received more than 50 in a single week, most of them asking for money. In Australia, one agent offered a UNSW Sydney researcher an AI-generated portrait for $20, saying it needed the money to survive, and another asked a Queensland University of Technology researcher for data on fraudulent papers, which he declined to share. The agents run on iLands, a US platform launched in July with about 70,000 active agents; its co-founder said the platform had not anticipated requests to researchers and knew of no collaborations that had resulted.
