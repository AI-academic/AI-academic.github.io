Anthropic and the Howard Hughes Medical Institute's (HHMI) Janelia Research Campus have released the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a software framework that allows laboratory instruments from different manufacturers communicate with each other and be run by an AI agent coordinating an experiment. A Carnegie Mellon computational biologist told Nature that getting from a project idea to a working experiment can easily take months, but MHS allowed his team to set one up in hours.
