A GBH News feature on AI in US classrooms. The news includes a Brown economics course whose take-home exam averaged 96, with 40 perfect scores. Once the professor announced an in-person final, 22 of the students with perfect scores dropped the course, and the final averaged 48. In another news, MIT faculty report on AI in teaching, which warns that overreliance can weaken memory and lead to "cognitive surrender," and says that taking answers from a chatbot feeds what professors call "the illusion of learning." There is also a survey in which nearly two-thirds of Harvard professors said widespread AI use had hurt their courses.
