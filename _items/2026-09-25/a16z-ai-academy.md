Venture firm Andreessen Horowitz has launched the Horowitz Andreessen Academy, a two-year, unaccredited, for-profit program in San Francisco for students aged 16 to 22. Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, Palantir and OpenAI are named as partners, and the adjunct faculty will be drawn from industry figures including Sam Altman. There will be no traditional grades, tests or homework; students take short classes and spend the rest of their time starting or working inside companies. Tuition is set to match an elite private university's, with the first year free, and a first cohort of about 50 is due to start in fall 2027. The founders pitch it as an alternative for ambitious students for whom, in an AI era, a traditional degree "may not be their best path to success."
