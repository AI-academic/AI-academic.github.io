Venture firm Andreessen Horowitz has launched the Horowitz Andreessen Academy, a two-year, unaccredited, for-profit program in San Francisco for students aged 16 to 22. Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, Palantir and OpenAI are named as partners, and the adjunct faculty will be drawn from industry figures including Sam Altman. There will be no traditional grades, tests or homework; students take short classes and spend the rest of their time starting or working inside companies. Tuition will be similar to an elite private university. The founders pitch the Academy as an alternative for ambitious students for whom a traditional degree may not offer as much success.
