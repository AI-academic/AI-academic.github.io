Inside Higher Ed's 2026 survey of chief academic officers, run with Hanover Research, drew 376 responses, mostly from US public and private nonprofit institutions. Roughly seven in ten provosts use AI in their own work at least once a week, most often to summarize documents and reports, prepare presentations and meetings and draft communications. Institutional adoption is much smaller. Roughly one in ten said their institution has a centralized AI strategy, and reported departmental workflow gains from AI investment and institution-wide operational change. In most cases, barriers were skills and staff capacity and faculty and staff resistance.
