Anthropic has introduced a life-sciences research group with its own Bay Area wet lab, where human scientists do all the bench work. Company said that about 950 Claude agents searched more than 200,000 reverse transcriptases (enzymes) in 21 hours and turned up a previously undescribed enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats, which it calls array-associated reverse transcriptases. The lab has expressed and characterized the protein, but its biological function remains unknown. The work is a preprint. Anthropic's CEO, Dario Amodei, acknowledged that a similar enzyme system was earlier reported by Stanford researchers.
