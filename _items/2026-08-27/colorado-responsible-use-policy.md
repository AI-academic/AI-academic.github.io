University of Colorado Administrative Policy Statement was announced on August 26. It establishes campus and systemwide AI committees and requires review when an AI tool processes university data, integrates with university systems, or significantly shapes decisions affecting individuals. It also requires disclosure when generative AI contributes to high-impact official outputs and holds human users accountable for reviewing outputs. The policy preserves faculty's principal responsibility for pedagogy, curriculum, research, scholarly work, and academic ethics, and leaves coursework guidance to academic units through shared governance.
