McMaster announced that five researchers from U.S. universities will join as Eddie Goldenberg Research Chairs of Canada, each supported by $8 million over eight years, and that the university received five Canada Impact+ Emerging Leaders awards totalling $3 million over six years. The chairs are expected to arrive between September 2026 and July 2027, subject to McMaster governance approvals. Their programs span radiopharmaceuticals, musculoskeletal health, sustainable innovation, and advanced materials; James LeBeau's chair will use quantitative and AI-enabled electron microscopy to study materials for batteries, semiconductors, and quantum devices.
