The U.S. National Science Foundation announced a five-year, $90 million investment in three new Science and Technology Centers, each expected to receive about $6 million annually during the initial term and potentially compete for five additional years. The Michigan State-led TEMPEST centre will combine experiments, high-performance computing, mathematical modelling, and AI to build physics-grounded turbulence models. The University of Texas at Austin-led Center for Human and Robot Co-Adaptation will combine robotics, AI, and human factors to study secure, effective adaptation between people and embodied systems. A third, Northwestern-led centre will focus on three-dimensional genome organization and engineering.
