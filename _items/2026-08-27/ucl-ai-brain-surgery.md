University College London reported that an in-house AI system analyzed a live endoscopic video feed during pituitary-tumour surgery and highlighted critical anatomy for the surgical team. The procedure was the first patient use in an ongoing clinical trial; UCL says the tumour was removed and the patient's vision improved. The system was trained and evaluated on annotated surgical videos and ran on an NVIDIA Clara IGX platform. Evidence released so far is an institutional account of one patient, not peer-reviewed trial results.
