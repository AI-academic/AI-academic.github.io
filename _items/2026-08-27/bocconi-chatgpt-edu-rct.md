A preregistered randomized controlled trial involving 1,053 first-year business students at Bocconi University (Italy) found that students who had access to ChatGPT Edu performed substantially better on a short business-recommendation task. By contrast, a single-session causal-reasoning exercise did not improve overall scores, although it did lead students to produce more varied solutions and to focus more explicitly on underlying mechanisms. The study is a working paper and has not yet been peer reviewed, and several of the authors are OpenAI employees or contractors.
