The University of Waterloo reported that an alumnus's $500,000 investment established the Sinnathamby Professorship in AI for Sustainable Solutions. John Quilty's graduate research team is developing machine-learning models for river-flow forecasting, reservoir operations, drinking-water contamination, and rare high-impact floods. Waterloo says the support enabled student recruitment and that results are already contributing to a flood-forecasting partnership with Natural Resources Canada.
