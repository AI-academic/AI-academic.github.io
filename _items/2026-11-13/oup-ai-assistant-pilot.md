Oxford University Press is piloting a link between its scholarly content and AI research assistants, so that licensed scholarship stays discoverable as researchers move from search engines to AI tools. Selected researchers and students at Harvard Library, the Wharton School, and the University of Oxford can query peer-reviewed content their institution already subscribes to through an assistant such as ChatGPT Edu, with answers citing sources and linking back to the Oxford Academic platform. The link is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, a standard way of plugging an AI assistant into an outside data source, built with OUP's platform provider, Silverchair. The pilot runs from October to December 2026 and tests whether researchers want such connections, whether trusted sources improve AI answers and how publisher citations change the way researchers verify sources. OUP will examine the usage data to judge whether publisher connectors are viable, and each institution will decide what role they should play in its research.
