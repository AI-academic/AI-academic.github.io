Google is extending its free Google AI Educator Series to Brazil, Mexico, Spain and Australia in the coming months, and to sub-Saharan Africa. It already runs the Series in the US, India, South Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines, the UK and Ireland. School teachers and university faculty complete on-demand sessions of up to 45 minutes on using Gemini for teaching, assessment and administration, earning badges with routes to college and continuing-education credit. Higher-education paths cover cognitive engagement, inclusive design, student support, agentic workflows and institutional impact.
