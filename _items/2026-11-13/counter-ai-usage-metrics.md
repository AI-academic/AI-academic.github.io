When an AI assistant reads a journal article on a researcher's behalf, should that count as a use? The question matters because library subscriptions, publisher negotiations and impact reports all rest on COUNTER Metrics (the standard behind scholarly usage statistics), built around the human "click." COUNTER's response comes in two phases: a best practice, issued in April 2026, separates human readers from machine activity in publishers' own AI tools, with new counts recording whether access came from a person or an agent. A second phase extends this to outside AI assistants that draw on publisher content, tracking whether material is retrieved, cited, and displayed in an AI's answer.
