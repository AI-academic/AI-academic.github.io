Concordia University has launched an Artificial Intelligence Engagement Framework as the next phase of its work on AI after the guiding principles developed by its AI Advisory Group. Working groups of faculty, staff and students will examine AI across teaching, research, university operations and governance, identifying opportunities, concerns and implications for institutional policy. Their recommendations go to a steering committee, which will produce an AI engagement plan that sets priorities and helps build a governance structure for the coming years. The announcement does not name the committee's members, the number of working groups or a timeline.
