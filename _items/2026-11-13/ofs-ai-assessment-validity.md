England's Office for Students (OfS) published research with Advance HE on how AI is changing higher education, alongside effective-practice guidance and a statement of next steps. The regulator says it "will not introduce new regulations" on AI. Instead, it expects providers to ensure assessments remain valid measures of students' knowledge and skills, which will often mean secure, AI-free assessment, and it can impose registration conditions, monetary penalties or funding suspensions where they fall short. The research found that some unsupervised assessments can be completed to a high standard with AI and little student effort, that AI detection tools are not consistently reliable, and that adoption has been driven mainly by individual staff rather than institutional strategy. Participants opposed new AI-specific rules, asking instead for clarity on how existing ones apply.
