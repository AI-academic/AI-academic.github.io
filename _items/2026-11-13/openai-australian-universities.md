OpenAI's expanded collaborations with four Australian universities give students and staff access to ChatGPT Edu and its Codex coding agent, with AI increasingly built into courses. UNSW Sydney is rolling ChatGPT Edu out to more than 80,000 students and staff and building generative-AI foundations and Codex into courses, alongside a "Can, Can't, Must" framework that lets educators set whether AI is permitted, prohibited or required in each assessment. La Trobe University already gives all 3,500 staff access, plans universal access for its 45,000 students and staff in early 2027, and will build AI skills into 50 priority first-year subjects. The University of Sydney will extend access to all students from the first semester of 2027 after a trial, and Adelaide University is among the first institutions using OpenAI's Daybreak Blue for authorised defensive security work. No pricing was disclosed.
