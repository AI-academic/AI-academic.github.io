The Australian government announced that forthcoming AI Standards legislation will impose mandatory safety and security obligations on all frontier AI companies that want to operate in Australia, enforced by a new targeted AI safety regulator able to levy significant penalties. Companies would have to maintain security controls across development and deployment, identify and manage serious risks, allow independent evaluation of their models, notify the government rapidly of incidents, publish safety and security frameworks, and keep pace with updated standards. The regulator would draw on the Australian AI Safety Institute for technical expertise; penalty amounts, timelines and consultation details are still to come.
