Ohio State University and Microsoft announced a five-year partnership spanning clinical care, education and research at the university and its Wexner Medical Center. Its most concrete commitment is an ambition to move roughly 70–80% of Ohio State's storage and compute, including digital pathology and imaging, to Microsoft Azure, with the electronic health record also backed up there. A governed Azure research environment could give investigators access to de-identified clinical data, and Ohio State is to become a Microsoft Training Services Provider, extending Microsoft training and certifications to students, faculty, staff, caregivers and community members. The deal follows Ohio State's September partnership with Google Cloud, which brought Gemini Enterprise campus-wide, Google DeepMind research collaborations and an on-campus AI hub.
