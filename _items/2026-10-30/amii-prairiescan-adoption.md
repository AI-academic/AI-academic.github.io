Prairies Economic Development Canada is adding $2.5 million to Amii's AI-adoption initiative, taking total federal investment to $5.6 million and allowing Amii to work directly with 25 new businesses across Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba. Since the 2024 launch Amii has worked with 30 regional SMEs. Delivery runs through Amii's "Advanced Technology Projects", in which companies work with Amii scientists and embedded machine-learning residents to build bespoke AI systems, with initial engagements in energy, agriculture and manufacturing.
