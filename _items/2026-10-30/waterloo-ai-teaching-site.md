The University of Waterloo announced a new institution-wide AI and Teaching website for instructors, as part of what Waterloo calls its "GenAI Teaching Enablement Project". The site has six sections: Getting Started, Assessing Student Learning, Teaching Students about AI, Course and Curriculum Design, Examples and Emerging Practices, and Support.

The site shows what an instructor-facing AI resource can look like at institutional scale. It pairs a list of approved tools with assessment redesign guidance and funding opportunities for teaching projects, rather than treating AI guidance as a standalone policy page.
