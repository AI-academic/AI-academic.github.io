The initiative is administered by Cornell's College of Arts and Sciences and funded in its first year through the Cornell AI Initiative plus a gift. Five postdoctoral fellows will serve up to two years, researching AI's social, political, ethical, cultural and economic effects and teaching undergraduate and graduate courses. Alongside Distinguished Visitors, five to six AI Practitioners-in-Residence from industry, government, civil society and the cultural and creative sectors will each spend one to two weeks on campus. A university-wide **AI & Society minor** is under development, drawing on political science, philosophy, history, literature, economics and sociology, and there will also be an annual symposium and research workshops.
