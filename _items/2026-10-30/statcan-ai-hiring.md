Statistics Canada researchers used Labour Force Survey data from January 2006 to August 2026 to track hiring and job separation rates for employees aged 15 to 64, grouping occupations by potential AI exposure and by whether AI would more likely complement or substitute for the work. The authors' conclusion: "There is no broad evidence that the rise of AI has led to substantial decreases in hiring or increases in job separation rates in Canada so far." Among 15-to-29-year-olds, the hiring rate in coding-intensive jobs fell from 2.5% in the six months to November 2022 to 1.9% in the six months to August 2026, but it also fell in jobs with low AI exposure, from 4.0% to 3.3%. The authors note that hiring began falling before ChatGPT's launch, and that it is unclear whether lower hiring reflects AI or other forces such as population shifts, trade shocks and post-pandemic adjustment.
