Purdue University announced that it will become the first US university to provide Google Tensor Processing Units through a campus computing program. Developed with Google Public Sector, Cirrascale and DDN, the installation is expected to increase Purdue's AI computing capacity by 30% and become available to initial users in January 2027; the capacity estimate is Purdue's, and no chip counts or costs were disclosed. Purdue will house the accelerators in its renovated Mathematical Sciences Building Data Center and integrate them into its community-cluster model while retaining institutional control over data, security and governance.
