The Budget was delivered on 28 October. Its AI measures include £120 million more for the AI Security Institute, £165 million for a new National AI Biosecurity Initiative and £140 million more for the Sovereign AI Unit; industry association techUK counts the package at more than £700 million, while IT Pro reports more than £600 million. On talent, the **Graduate visa will be two years, rather than the 18 months due from January 2027, but only for graduates of high-compliance universities with strong labour market outcomes**, and those criteria are not yet set. The High Potential Individual route expands to the top 150 global institutions, more businesses will be sought for the Global Talent endorsed-funder pathway, Migration Advisory Committee recommendations on salary thresholds apply from spring 2027, and the UK will participate in Erasmus+ from 1 January 2027.
