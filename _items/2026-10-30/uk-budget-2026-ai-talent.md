The Budget was delivered on 28 October. Its AI measures include £120 million more for the AI Security Institute, £165 million for a new National AI Biosecurity Initiative and £140 million more for the Sovereign AI Unit. Industry association techUK counts the package at more than £700 million, while IT Pro reports more than £600 million.
