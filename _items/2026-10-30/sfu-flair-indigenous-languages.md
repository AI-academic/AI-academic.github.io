First Languages AI Reality, housed at Simon Fraser University's Digital Democracies Institute and Cedar Supercomputing Centre, is working with the Gwich'in, Kwakwaka'wakw, Makah and Yawanawá communities to develop community-led language curricula, curated audio-text datasets and smaller speech-recognition and learning tools that can operate locally. SFU states that participating communities retain ownership of the data and outputs they co-develop. Cisco donated hardware for the project's server cluster; the learning tools remain in development.
