Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that the federal government plans to invest up to $1.6 billion in Cohere on a commercial, staged basis, taking an equity stake alongside private investors NVIDIA, Bell and Germany's Schwarz Group. The stated conditions are that Cohere remains Canadian-incorporated and headquartered; **invests at least $1.7 billion in new Canadian computing infrastructure by 2030**; keeps data in Canada under Canadian law; creates at least 1,000 new high-paying Canadian technical jobs; **forms research partnerships with Canadian institutions and creates opportunities for students and recent graduates**; continues working with the Canadian AI Safety Institute; and reports regularly on these commitments.
