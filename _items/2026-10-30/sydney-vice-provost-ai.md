The University of Sydney appointed Engineering Dean Hesham El Gamal to the additional role of Vice-Provost (AI Implementation), effective immediately. The university says the role will coordinate human-centred adoption and governance across faculties, schools and portfolios as Sydney moves from strategy to implementation. Its remit includes the university-wide ChatGPT Edu partnership and associated capability development, alongside education, research, operations and public-interest work.
