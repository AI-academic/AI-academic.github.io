Waterloo Engineering alumnus Albert (Feng) Yang has given $1 million to create the Yang Family Professorship, with his former PhD supervisor, Distinguished Professor Emeritus Andrew Wong, as its first holder. The fund supports AI work in fields including robotics, health care, manufacturing and information systems, with the goal of measurable gains in performance, safety and user benefit.
