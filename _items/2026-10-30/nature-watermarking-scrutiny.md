Responding to a *Nature* news story on Anthropic's invisible watermarks, Weibin Hu and Xueru Zhang argue that watermarking efforts for AI-generated content must encourage independent public scrutiny. They warn that watermarks can themselves become targets: in tests of earlier schemes, attackers spoofed or removed them for less than US$50, with a success rate of roughly 80%.
