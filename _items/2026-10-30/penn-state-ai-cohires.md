The Institute for Computational and Data Sciences (ICDS) announced 26 co-hires at University Park and Penn State Harrisburg, including a "Capital Group" cohort partnering Harrisburg with the College of Medicine in Hershey, Dickinson Law and the Clinical and Translational Science Institute. Research focus areas are core AI expertise, efficient solutions to complex scientific problems, rural health care, AI policy and leadership, and ethical AI literacy. A second cluster round opens tenure-track and tenured searches in **AI + materials, AI + health, and foundations of AI**. Penn State has a named vice provost for AI, Vasant Honavar.
