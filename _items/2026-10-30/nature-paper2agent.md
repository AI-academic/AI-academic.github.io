A Stanford-led team describes a framework in which multiple AI agents read a paper and its codebase and build a Model Context Protocol server, tested and refined iteratively. The resulting "paper MCP" can be attached to a chat agent, letting a reader query the paper's methods conversationally and execute them. The authors report case studies on AlphaGenome for genomic variant interpretation and on Scanpy and TISSUE for single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, stating that the agents reproduce the original published results and also handle new queries. Further agents collaborated to prioritize a causal gene for psoriasis, and an "AI co-scientist" prioritized candidate causal variants across genetic loci linked to ADHD. The open-access paper was published online on 16 September and appears in the 29 October issue.
