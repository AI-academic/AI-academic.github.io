Canada's 14 privacy regulators have jointly called for AI in health care and health research to be governed by stronger laws and clearer research ethics rules. For universities, the main points are a request to update TCPS 2 for AI and a call to strengthen the governance, resources and training of research ethics boards. Researchers are also asked to refine risk-benefit assessments and publicly share their results.
