uOttawa has established the Centre for Applied Artificial Intelligence (CAAI) within its Faculty of Engineering, bringing together about 50 experts to build AI solutions for real-world problems and offering partner organizations direct access to its researchers, engineers and applied-AI expertise. Director Lionel Briand frames it around trust: "Canada needs AI that earns trust in labs, in workplaces, and in everyday life". The university's move to position the applied-AI centre as an industry front door positions it well in the Ontario market for AI partnership revenue.
