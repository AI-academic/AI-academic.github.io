UdeM's "Brave the Way" campaign reached its $1 billion target less than three years after its public launch, which the university says is the largest sum ever raised by a French-language university worldwide. Funds raised so far have supported 781 scholarships, 71 research chairs and initiatives including Millénium Québecor. The university will continue the campaign, with priorities including student spaces and innovative solutions in AI, and aims to strengthen its standing among leading research universities. Vice-Rector Michael Pecho said the milestone makes possible projects that, not long ago, "seemed unfeasible."
