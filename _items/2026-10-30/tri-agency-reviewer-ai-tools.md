CIHR, NSERC, SSHRC and the Canada Foundation for Innovation have updated their guidance on generative AI in the development and review of research funding applications. The main change is that institutionally procured and deployed generative AI tools are now not permitted for reviewers carrying out review work, which goes beyond the earlier prohibition on publicly available online tools. The FAQ now has separate applicant and reviewer sections, so permitted and prohibited uses are easier to find. The existing principles remain: applicants are accountable for the full contents of their applications, and privacy, confidentiality, data security and intellectual property must be prioritized. The guidance also warns that uploading application material, including draft reviews, into generative AI tools risks unauthorized disclosure.
