Cornell University (USA) received a US$750,000 National Science Foundation grant for a three-faculty team spanning biomedical engineering, physics and biology to build and test the Automated Critical Thinking Reflection tool, a generative-AI system designed to prompt personalised student reflection on lectures and lab work across physics, biology and engineering courses. The project is framed around a dual risk: that using AI can undermine students' reasoning, and that lacking AI literacy can too.
