Florida's Board of Governors, acting on its Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity Task Force, issued a public notice of intent to require every public university and college in the state to adopt an AI policy, covering whether faculty must disclose AI use, whether students must, and what student AI use is permitted in coursework. Final approval is still pending, and the Florida Department of Education was expected to consider a parallel rule for the public college sector the following week. At the time of reporting, of Florida's 12 universities and 28 public colleges only 8 had produced AI guidance of any kind, and just 2 that includes the University of Florida had adopted a formal policy. A mandate to have a policy is not a mandate about what the policy says, so it shouldn't be a huge issue for institutions.
