The multi-year OpenAI agreement, reported here last week, is now posted on the university's news site along with new teaching details. All students and staff will receive free, supported access to ChatGPT Edu through a staged rollout guided by the university's AI Framework, with no institutional data used for model training. Its assessment model runs two lanes: secure supervised work that demonstrates independent capability, and open unsupervised work that permits transparent AI use. The university-developed [Cogniti platform](https://cogniti.ai/) remains the preferred classroom tool, offering personalised tutoring, feedback and role-play, with ChatGPT Edu supplementing rather than replacing it. The $1 million AUD AI Sustainability Research and Innovation Fund will be directed at measuring and reducing AI's environmental impacts including work on energy-efficient photonic chips. The detail worth carrying forward is that a general-purpose vendor tool was deliberately placed underneath a purpose-built teaching platform rather than over it.
