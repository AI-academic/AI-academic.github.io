Anthropic launched a beta Life Sciences Verification Program for academic labs, startups, pharmaceutical companies and other institutions whose biology work is restricted by its general model safeguards. Verified organisations can receive team-wide Standard Use grants, which apply classifiers tuned to be more permissive for science tasks and renew annually, or project-specific High-risk Use grants, which lift life-sciences safeguards for a named project and renew every six months. 
Access is available only on Claude for Enterprise and Team plans plus the API — not on individual Pro or Max plans, not through third-party platforms, and not yet for organisations operating under a BAA for protected health information, which excludes much clinical work. 
Verification is described only as "a review of their research credentials, security standards, and ethical research oversight"; the announcement sets out no criteria, no applicant role, and nothing specific to non-US institutions. Vendor-operated verification of this kind will likely require a process for attesting to institutional oversight, a constraint for Canadian universities.
