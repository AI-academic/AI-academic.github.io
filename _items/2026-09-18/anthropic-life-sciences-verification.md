Anthropic launched a beta Life Sciences Verification Program for academic labs, startups, and other institutions whose biology work is restricted by its general model safeguards. Verified organisations can receive team-wide Standard Use access or project-specific High-risk Use access. Access is available only on Team and Enterprise plans plus the API — not on individual Pro or Max plans, not through third-party platforms. Also, the program is not yet available for much of clinical work. The announcement is yet to spell out criteria and applicant role, and there is no specific information for non-US institutions. This type of vendor-operated funding will likely require a process for attesting to institutional oversight, a constraint for Canadian universities.
