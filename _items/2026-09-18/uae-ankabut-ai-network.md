Telecom operator 'e& UAE' renewed its partnership with [Ankabut](https://www.ankabut.ae/), the UAE's national research and education network, to build a private high-performance network for AI model training, deep-learning inference and high-performance computing at research institutions. The release describes it as a "digital expressway" connecting universities directly to local AI hyperscalers and cloud environments, and onward to international research and education networks through Ankabut's NREN. It frames the work as underpinning the UAE's National AI Strategy. No partner universities are named, and no capacity, cost, or timeline figures are given. So, the announcement establishes intent rather than a deliverable. This also shows that campus AI capability is partly a networking issue that requires involvement of national research and education networks.
