A McMaster University researcher and PhD candidate in the Faculty of Health Sciences has founded EBAiM (Evidence-Based AI in Medicine), an international group convening clinicians, AI researchers, statisticians, and others. Its argument is that AI adoption in medicine is currently fragmented and largely unvalidated, and that adoption should be driven by evidence rather than by vendors. EBAiM has published a Chatbot Assessment Reporting Tool (CHART), for evaluating AI chatbots that summarise clinical evidence, and is now developing a consensus framework for validating generative AI systems in health applications internationally.
