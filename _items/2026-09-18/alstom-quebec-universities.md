The rail manufacturer Alstom announced an applied-research partnership with Université Laval, UQAM and ÉTS to apply AI and Industry 4.0 techniques to smart manufacturing, using real factory data. The work is funded through NSERC and Mitacs and will train graduate students and postdoctoral researchers.
