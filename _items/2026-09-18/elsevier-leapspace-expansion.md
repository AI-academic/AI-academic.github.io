Elsevier announced a large content expansion of LeapSpace, its AI-assisted workspace for literature review, study design and manuscript preparation, now drawing on more than 23 million full-text peer-reviewed articles and books plus 2.6 billion cited references from Scopus. Elsevier claims the corpus now accounts for 56% of recent global research citations and 42% of global research articles published since 2021. A new five-member independent advisory board, chaired by Chancellor's Professor Emeritus Jörg-Rüdiger Sack (Carleton University), was created to oversee algorithmic transparency and publisher-neutral ranking of content. LeapSpace is sold on institutional, corporate and individual tiers, with free access for academics and students.
