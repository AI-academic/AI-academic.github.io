Mila and DFKI, Germany's national AI research centre, signed a memorandum of understanding at the ALL IN conference in Montréal, committing to joint research on trustworthy and safe AI, agentic AI, and AI for the energy sector including smart grids and renewable forecasting, alongside researcher mobility and methodology exchange. The German federal minister and Canada's AI Minister welcomed the agreement as deepening scientific exchange with a G7 partner. DFKI chief executive Antonio Krüger framed the shared ground as "human-centric and trustworthy artificial intelligence," and Mila president Valérie Pisano called it an important next step. An MOU sets direction rather than funding, so what matters is which of the four named areas attracts a joint call first.
