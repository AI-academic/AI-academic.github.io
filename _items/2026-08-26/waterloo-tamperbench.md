Waterloo published an institutional account of TamperBench, a standardized framework used to evaluate 21 open-weight large language models across nine tampering threats. The underlying paper reports that current alignment-stage defences largely failed to withstand systematic attack sweeps. Waterloo said the work was recently presented at the ACM Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining. The paper itself was first submitted in February and revised in June; the new development in this briefing window is Waterloo's August 25 release following the conference presentation.
