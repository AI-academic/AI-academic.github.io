MIT's president and senior academic leaders released the final report of the Ad Hoc Committee on AI Use in Teaching, Learning, and Research Training. The committee recommends three broad directions: create "AI-aware" educational processes, including changes to learning goals, assessment, and policy; centre people, community, and the residential experience; and build processes, teams, and tools for continuous reflection and improvement. MIT also called for clear course-level AI-use policies, renewed emphasis on hands-on learning, guidance and pilot funding, and discipline-specific communities of practice for teaching and research.
