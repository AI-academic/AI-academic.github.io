UNESCO announced that Digital Learning Week, September 8-11 in Paris, will bring together more than 25 education ministers and other participants to adopt a joint ministerial statement on sustaining education as a common good in the age of AI. UNESCO also plans to release policy briefs on AI safety, sovereignty, procurement, cost models, and age-appropriateness, along with new survey data on AI trends in higher education. The announcement establishes the agenda and planned outputs; their substance is not yet available for assessment.
