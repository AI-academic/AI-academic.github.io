The University of Iowa set out three strategic directions for a campus-wide AI strategy: engaging with AI across every discipline, ensuring responsible and continually evolving expertise, and amplifying human potential. Implementation is already partly in place - rhetoric faculty have integrated AI literacy into general education courses reaching nearly all new undergraduates, an undergraduate Certificate in AI has launched, and the university provides protected institutional access to AI platforms with accompanying guidance. The strategy is backed by the AI Discovery Initiative, a three-year, $1-million program supporting up to 120 faculty and staff projects annually with platform credits, training, mentorship, computing resources and visiting scholar lectures. The strategy comes at a time when the delivery mechanisms are already running, which gives the document something to point at.
