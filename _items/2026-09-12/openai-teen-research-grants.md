OpenAI announced research grants of up to $1 million each, totalling up to $5 million, for independent work on how generative AI affects people aged 13 to 17. Proposals are welcome from any country, with priority given to countries with significant AI adoption; grants may support graduate students and postdoctoral researchers, and for-profit organizations are not prioritized. Projects involving minors will require ethics statements covering consent, privacy protection, safeguarding protocols and team expertise in child development. For research offices the practical questions are the familiar ones about industry-funded evidence on industry products — independence of publication, access to data, and what "independent" means when the funder is also the subject.
