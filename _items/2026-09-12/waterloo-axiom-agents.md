The Centre for Teaching Excellence at the University of Waterloo is funding "Axiom," a pilot giving students in six STEM courses access to course-specific AI agents, through a Learning Innovation and Teaching Enhancement (LITE) seed grant. Three distinct agents are deployed: a feedback agent delivering individualized responses to work produced in in-class learning activities, a self-testing agent supporting preparation for summative assessments, and an administrative agent answering course-information questions. The team is drawn from Physics and Astronomy and the Integrated Teaching Support Unit, and the project runs from September 2026 to February 2028. The design is worth noting: each agent is confined to one pedagogical job instead of being offered as a general assistant.
