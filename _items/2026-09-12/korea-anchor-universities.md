Korea's Education Ministry selected six universities as models to lead cross-regional workforce training under its "Anchor" framework, covering AI, semiconductors, energy and biotechnology and targeting roughly 14,000 trainees in total. Funding runs up to 15 billion won per year per model for four years, adjusted on performance, with a structure that lets 17 regional governments align training with local industry. Named corporate partners include SK hynix and Megazone. Selected universities will serve as regional training infrastructure with a standing mandate, which is different from the project-by-project grants normally seen elsewhere. The models also have different exposures if the sector forecast is wrong.
