The Ontario government announced $90 million for McMaster University to create 2,769 new funded student seats in programs aligned with "in-demand careers," with STEM, health care, education and AI named as priority fields. The money moves through provincial funded-seat policy rather than any AI-specific program, so what it signals is where the province expects enrolment to grow rather than a new institutional commitment on AI. The significant detail is the company AI now keeps: it is listed beside health care and education in the province's undergraduate capacity planning, which is where funded seats, not pilot projects, get decided.
