The Ontario government announced $90 million for McMaster University to create 2,769 new funded student seats in programs aligned with "in-demand careers," with STEM, health care, education, and AI named as priority fields. The money will come from provincial funded-seat policy rather than any AI-specific program. The funding is not a new institutional commitment on AI, rather it is meant to support enrolment growth in priority areas.
