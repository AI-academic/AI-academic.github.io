Google will establish a physical presence inside Ohio State University's Innovation District on the west campus, described by the university as one of the largest foundational partnerships between Google and any US university and as among the first Google spaces on any university campus. The arrangement gives students access to Google DeepMind research and personalized AI assistant tools, and includes what is described as a first-of-its-kind research-ambassador internship program run through Google Public Sector. It complements Ohio State's existing AI Fluency initiative, under which the university committed last year to embedding AI education across the undergraduate curriculum. A vendor with a building on campus is a different relationship from a vendor with a licensing agreement, and the governance questions that follow — over research direction, student data and exclusivity — scale accordingly.
