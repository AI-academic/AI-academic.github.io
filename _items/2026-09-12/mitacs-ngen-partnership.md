Mitacs and Next Generation Manufacturing Canada announced a two-year partnership to place students and recent graduates into industry-led advanced-manufacturing R&D across Canada, targeting roughly 60 internships valued at between $600,000 and $1.2 million. AI is listed as a priority sector alongside defence and security and quantum technologies, building on a 2022 memorandum of understanding between the two organizations. For universities the practical effect is a funded route for graduate students into applied AI work with manufacturers, on terms set jointly by a federal research-internship body and an industry-led cluster rather than by either institution or company alone.
