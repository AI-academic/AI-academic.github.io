Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada launched the National AI Literacy Initiative in Edmonton on 9 September 2026, a $13-million national partnership delivered with the Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute and described as a key deliverable under [Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadas-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy-ai-all). It reaches up to one million post-secondary students and runs three streams: Essentials for Students, Essentials for Educators, and Essentials for Canadians. Beginning September 21, 2026, post-secondary institutions can join a consortium and offer students a free three-hour course covering foundational knowledge, responsible use and workplace readiness. It is up to each institution to decide whether it wants to join the consortium.
