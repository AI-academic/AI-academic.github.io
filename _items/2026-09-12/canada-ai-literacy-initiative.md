Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada launched the National AI Literacy Initiative in Edmonton on 9 September 2026, a $13-million national partnership delivered with the Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute and described as a key deliverable under Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All. It reaches up to one million post-secondary students and runs three streams — Essentials for Students, Essentials for Educators, and Essentials for Canadians. Beginning 21 September 2026, post-secondary institutions can join a consortium and offer students a free three-hour course covering foundational knowledge, responsible use and workplace readiness; the first chapter of the educator curriculum releases the same day, with further chapters and the Essentials for Canadians stream rolling out through fall 2026. The announcement was made by Evan Solomon, Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation. Joining the consortium is a decision each institution has to make for itself, and the September start date makes it an immediate one.
