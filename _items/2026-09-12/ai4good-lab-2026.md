CIFAR published a feature on the AI4Good Lab, a seven-week program for women and gender-diverse participants run by Mila in partnership with CIFAR, the Vector Institute and Amii, profiling four award-winning projects from the 2026 cohort. The program was co-founded by Doina Precup, Canada CIFAR AI Chair at Mila. It remains one of the longer-running Canadian pipelines aimed at widening who builds AI systems rather than who is trained to use them.
