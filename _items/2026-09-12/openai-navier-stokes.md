OpenAI published a proposed analytical proof of the Navier–Stokes problem, stating that its solution establishes statements C and D in the [official Millennium Prize formulation](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/). The company says the work came from an internal model that is significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra. The model coordinated 10,000 agents that worked together for roughly 88 hours. Independent mathematical review remains incomplete. The company does not intend to claim the Millennium Prize. Questions have been raised about priority and possible influence from the work of two researchers, Tristan Buckmaster (NYU) and Levent Alpöge (Anthropic), who resolved related Euler equation problems twelve hours earlier, and Buckmaster has suggested OpenAI's agents may have benefited from his team's prior work, which was itself conducted using OpenAI's models.
