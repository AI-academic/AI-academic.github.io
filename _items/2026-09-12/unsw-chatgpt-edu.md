UNSW Sydney announced an expansion of its OpenAI partnership, extending ChatGPT Edu access — already covering professional and academic staff — to Higher Degree Research candidates and casual academic staff, with access for the full student body of more than 80,000 planned for the 2027 academic year. The university frames the deployment as one of the largest AI-in-education rollouts in the Asia-Pacific and pairs it with a ["Can, Can't, Must" framework](https://www.education.unsw.edu.au/news-events/news/permissions-purpose-program-level-approach-assessment-ai-integrated-university) governing AI use in assessment, alongside workforce-aligned curriculum guidance and research support tools. This is an incremental expansion of a deal first announced in September 2025 rather than a new agreement. The assessment framework, which sets permissions at program level rather than leaving each course to improvise, is the part other universities are likelier to borrow.
