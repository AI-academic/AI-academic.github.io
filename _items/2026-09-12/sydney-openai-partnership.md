The University of Sydney has signed a multi-year agreement with OpenAI for campus-wide ChatGPT Edu access, reported by the student newspaper Honi Soit from an official announcement. The university is also committing $1 million to an "AI Sustainability Research and Innovation Fund" and bundling several endorsed tools, among them Cogniti, Harvey AI, Microsoft Copilot, ServiceNow Now Assist and Zoom AI Companion. Coming two days after UNSW's expansion, it leaves both of Sydney's largest universities running institution-wide OpenAI deployments, with the endorsed-tool list — rather than a single assistant — as the distinguishing feature of this one.
