UK Research and Innovation announced eight measures to modernise grant assessment, taking effect from October 2026. The AI element begins as a retrospective trial comparing AI-generated conclusions against funding decisions actually made, with UKRI committing to "keeping expert human judgement at the heart of every decision" and to monitoring equality, diversity and inclusion implications under its Public Sector Equality Duty. UKRI states that it is working with Canada, Namibia and Switzerland on desk-rejection approaches, and with the United States, Germany and Canada on Distributed Peer Review. The Canadian involvement is the part worth tracking: methods trialled in this consortium tend to surface next in the agencies Canadian researchers actually apply to.
