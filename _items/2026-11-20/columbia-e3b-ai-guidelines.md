Columbia University's Department of Ecology, Evolution and Environmental Biology has distributed generative AI guidelines that treat educational work (classes, degree programs and dissertations) separately from other research. Uses are classed as "never," "usually not" or "usually fine"; those in the middle category need explicit permission from an instructor or adviser and must be disclosed in writing. Instructors and advisers can give more specific direction, while university, journal and funder policies continue to apply. The department emphasizes checking all AI output, including reading every reference an AI tool supplies.
