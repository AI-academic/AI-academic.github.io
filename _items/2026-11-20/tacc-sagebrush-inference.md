The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) at the University of Texas at Austin is building Sagebrush, an NSF-funded AI inference service that runs continuously, rather than as queued batch jobs, to give the US research community access to open AI models, agentic tools and domain-specific workflows. The service will host general AI queries and retrieval-augmented services tuned to particular research domains, including one built on supercomputing documentation and code to support HPC code development and failure analysis. The grant began in September 2026, with a two-year experimental phase to be followed by three years in production.
