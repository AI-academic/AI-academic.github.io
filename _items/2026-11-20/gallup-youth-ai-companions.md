Gallup, working with the Boston Children's Hospital Digital Wellness Lab, surveyed people aged 15 to 22 across 16 countries between May and August 2026. Since many in this age group are incoming or current undergraduates, the results are relevant to student well-being and counseling services. Survey results showed that roughly one-third reported having used AI as a companion or friend, rising to half among daily AI users; more than 80% had used AI to look up information and two-third had asked it for advice, while about 93% reported at least one strong in-person friendship. Those using AI as a companion showed 70% higher adjusted odds of higher loneliness, and Gallup mentioned that the direction of cause and effect is unclear. There are wide variation in data from different countries: 45% of Australian respondents with AI companions rated the relationship high quality, against just 12% in Poland.
