Gallup, working with the Boston Children's Hospital Digital Wellness Lab, surveyed people aged 15 to 22 across 16 countries between May and August 2026. A median of 33% reported having used AI as a companion or friend, rising to 50% among daily AI users; 82% had used AI to look up information and 68% had asked it for advice, while about 93% reported at least one strong in-person friendship. Those using AI as a companion showed 70% higher adjusted odds of higher loneliness, and Gallup states explicitly that the direction of cause and effect is unclear. National variation was wide: 45% of Australian respondents with AI companions rated the relationship high quality, against 12% in Poland. Many in this age group are incoming or current undergraduates, which makes the study relevant to student well-being and counselling services.
