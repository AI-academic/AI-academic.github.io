Brown University has hired Michael Littman, a longtime Brown computer science professor recruited back from the US National Science Foundation, as its associate provost for artificial intelligence. Fewer than half of Brown's courses have a syllabus policy stating when students may use AI, and Littman has built a tool that helps faculty generate a customized AI policy for their own course. Brown now offers AI-focused courses in history, engineering and medicine alongside computer science, while some courses have moved the other way, keeping devices out of the classroom and using paper course packets that students mark up by hand.
