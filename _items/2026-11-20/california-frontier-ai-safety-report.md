A report from California's Government Operations Agency, released by Governor Newsom's office on 18 November, recommends licensing independent organizations to test and monitor the largest AI developers' models, with state power to order an immediate shutdown on credible evidence of an imminent catastrophic risk. For universities, it proposes funding CalCompute, a public computing cluster with capacity reserved for independent safety and security research, and names academic institutions among the outside experts that could do the testing. The recommendations aren't law yet, and will be considered by lawmakers when they return in December.
