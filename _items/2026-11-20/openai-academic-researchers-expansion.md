OpenAI is expanding its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program, launched as a pilot in July, with a goal of giving up to 200,000 researchers worldwide free access. The next round opens on 23 November, with 50,000 grants awarded on a first-come, first-served basis; each gives the recipient and up to two collaborators free ChatGPT Plus for twelve months. Research faculty and postdoctoral researchers in all regions except China and Hong Kong are eligible, so Canadian researchers qualify. OpenAI says that by default it does not use data shared through the program to train models. It reports that 20,000 researchers in 33 countries joined during the pilot, and that in a survey by its own economic research team, more than 1,800 pilot users *estimated* the same work at the same quality would have taken nearly twice as long without AI.
