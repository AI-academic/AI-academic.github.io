MIT's Human Insight Collaborative (MITHIC), launched in 2024 and co-chaired by the dean of its School of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences and the provost, has funded 67 projects across 31 MIT units with US$5.5 million, about US$82,000 per project on average. Roughly a third focus on AI and society. They include research on how chatbots shape the political information voters receive and on AI's effects on thinking, along with new teaching: a course on humane user-experience design, a revived Computing and Society concentration, and faculty workshops on generative AI and writing.
