A week after deciding not to write new AI rules but to enforce assessment validity, England's Office for Students (OfS) has told universities that assessment needs urgent attention, warning that some assessments can now be completed with AI without students showing the knowledge and skills they are meant to demonstrate. Its joint research with Advance HE cites HEPI data showing 94% of students use generative AI in assessed work. The regulator wants institutions to review whether assessments that allow AI still measure what they claim to, and to use closed, AI-free formats such as exams, presentations or practicals where needed. Ofs pointed to the University of Bath's "two lane" model, in which an assessment is either secure and AI-free or designed on the assumption that students will use AI. Universities will have to show in their quality submissions that their assessments remain valid in the context of AI.
