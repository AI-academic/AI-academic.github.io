The University of Warsaw received a US$1 million Google.org grant, plus US$100,000 in Google Cloud credits, for its two-year Efficient AI-for-Science project, running in 2027 and 2028. It plans an open-source toolkit for adapting existing AI models to scientific data, a fellowship pairing machine-learning doctoral researchers with natural-science research teams, and workshops and bootcamps for researchers across Central and Eastern Europe.
