The Council of the European Union approved conclusions calling for AI skills across education and lifelong learning, including critically evaluating AI outputs, knowing when not to use AI, educator development and whole-institution approaches to AI policy. The conclusions encourage higher education providers to update and develop programs for the advanced AI skills emerging occupations need, and invite member states to evaluate AI's educational impacts, including on existing inequalities. These are Council conclusions, a statement of agreed position, not a new binding regulation.
