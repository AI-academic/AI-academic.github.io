University of Toronto Provost Trevor Young has appointed Professor Cristina Amon as Provostial Advisor on Computing, AI, and Data Science. Professor Amon will consult across the university and lead development of a strategic framework covering interdisciplinary collaboration, academic programming, research, external engagement, AI for Science, innovation and commercialization. The work will end in a report to the provost with recommendations for the university's future academic planning.
