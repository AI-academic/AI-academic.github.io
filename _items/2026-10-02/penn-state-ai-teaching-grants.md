Penn State has opened a microgrant program offering up to 50 awards of $1,000 for AI experiments in spring 2027 teaching, and a transformation program funding six to 10 projects of up to $50,000 to redesign large courses or degree programs in 2027-28. The larger projects must pair AI with at least one of experiential learning, reimagined assessment or civic learning. Applicants must complete the university's AI Essentials training and have the support of their academic unit head.
