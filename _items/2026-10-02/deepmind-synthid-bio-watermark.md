A Nature paper from Google DeepMind describes SynthIDBio, two methods for embedding detectable watermarks in AI-designed protein sequences and predicted structures. Binders designed against three targets, including the SARS-CoV-2 receptor-binding domain, kept binding affinities the authors describe as comparable with unwatermarked designs, and the watermark was detected with near-perfect accuracy. The authors present it as a proof of concept for biosecurity and for tracing where a protein design came from.
