From October 1, arXiv limits each submitter to two new submissions per calendar month and three active submissions at once; co-authors on a paper are not counted against the limit. arXiv received 40,363 submissions in September 2026, close to double the 20,569 of September 2024, and its moderators report "a marked increase in dense, AI-written papers," warning that AI tools let authors flood repositories with low-value work. arXiv continues to permit AI as a research tool where its use is disclosed and the work meets its scholarly standards.
