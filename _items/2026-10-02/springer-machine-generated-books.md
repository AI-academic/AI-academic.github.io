In an Inside Higher Ed opinion piece, University of California Irvine professor Daniel Gross argues that AI-generated scholarly books are reaching university libraries through routine publisher purchases at the University of California as the California Digital Library buys Springer's e-book front list for all campuses. He notes that Springer Nature has published machine-generated literature overviews since 2021. Dr. Gross offers recommendations including faculty awareness, collective negotiation with publishers,  and preferential support for publishers that maintain scholarly standards.
