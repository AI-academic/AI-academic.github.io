UK Research and Innovation has opened a £5 million competition for exploratory projects pairing AI specialists with researchers in engineering biology, advanced materials, quantum technologies, medical research or fusion energy. Eligible UK research organizations can seek up to £250,000 at full economic cost, funded at 80%, for projects of up to one year. The program looks for interdisciplinary capability, prototypes, roadmaps and evidence for future AI-enabled research; projects mainly about advancing AI methods, without a clear link to a research domain, are out of scope.
