Microsoft Research has introduced Quine, an early-stage system combining a multimodal model of biology with scientific tools, the literature and the scientists using them. Microsoft reported that Quine worked with Broad Institute researchers on pancreatic to identify compounds for priority testing, and that its high ranked candidates produced maximum impact in lab assays. Access is limited for now to select research collaborations and a first cohort of Quine Fellows, for which applications are open. Microsoft describes Quine as experimental research technology, not for clinical use, whose outputs require expert review and experimental validation.
