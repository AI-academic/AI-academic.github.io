At the EDUCAUSE annual conference, Google.org announced support for three higher-education initiatives. One builds Grow with Google resources and AI lessons into professional development for educators at 30 universities, through the Coalition of Urban Serving Universities and APLU, to improve support for adult learners; a second, with the same coalition, will study career-focused community-based learning models over 15 months; and a third, with Education Design Lab, will open a national competition for state community-college systems in early 2027. The announcement gives no funding amounts or selection criteria.
