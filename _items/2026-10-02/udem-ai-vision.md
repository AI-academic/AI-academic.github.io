Université de Montréal has published a university-wide vision for artificial intelligence, developed through teaching, research and operations panels set up in 2024-25, communities of practice, deans, governance bodies and student surveys on AI use. Its six principles address human-centred use, skills and critical thinking, sovereignty and accessibility, reliability, security and trust, responsibility and ethics, and informed decisions and social impact. The vision is already guiding the university's tools and practical guidelines, including dedicated guidance on generative AI in administrative work.
