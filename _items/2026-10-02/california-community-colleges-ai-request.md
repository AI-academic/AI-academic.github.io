The California Community Colleges system has put a $195M AI package into its 2027-28 state budget request for its 116 colleges. The largest piece is $100M in one-time funding for technology infrastructure; another $40M for professional development would include a centralized professional learning portal and system-wide frameworks for AI ethics and data governance. The system argues that, without a deliberate strategy, AI adoption will be uneven across colleges, opening gaps in access, capacity, cybersecurity and student outcomes from one region to another. This is a clear example of a sector arguing that the equity risk of AI is between institutions, not only between students.
