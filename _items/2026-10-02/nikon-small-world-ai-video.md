Nature reports a controversy over the winning entry in Nikon's Small World in Motion competition, a microscopy video by Ning Xu, after it emerged that the footage had been processed with AI. On September 22, Nikon amended its description of the entry to disclose that an "unsupervised" AI model assisted with post-processing; Xu says the AI only distinguished and coloured structures and did not generate the footage or the motion in it. Some researchers question whether structures shown in the video occur in biology at all. Concerns have been raised that scientific images are not simply works of art; they are also real data.
