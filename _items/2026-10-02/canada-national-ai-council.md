Prime Minister Mark Carney has launched a 13-member National Council on Artificial Intelligence to provide independent advice on implementing and evolving Canada's AI for All strategy. The council's mandate spans adoption, national champions, sovereign infrastructure, safety, democracy, talent and research. Members include university-based researchers alongside industry, investment and public-sector leaders. A small Privy Council Office team will support the council, while ministers and departments remain accountable for decisions and implementation.
