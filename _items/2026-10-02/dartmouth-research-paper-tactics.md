Dartmouth political scientists Brendan Nyhan and Jeffrey Friedman argue in the Chronicle of Higher Education that research-paper assignments can survive generative AI if three measures are used together: requiring students to draft the entire paper in a shared Google Doc so the instructor can see the writing process, pairing the paper with an oral or written exam on its content, and using AI-detection software. They contend that, combined with clear policies and accountability, these measures can deter AI misuse.
