[EDUCAUSE](https://www.educause.edu/), [Quality Matters](https://www.qualitymatters.org/), and [Encoura Eduventures Research](https://www.encoura.org/eduventures-research/) collaborated to release the 2026 Changing Landscape of Online Education (CHLOE) Conversations series, drawing on six focus groups with 30 online-learning leaders from community colleges and public and private four-year institutions. The reports look at generative AI in the broader context of online-program strategy, quality, accessibility, student support, and the use of data.
