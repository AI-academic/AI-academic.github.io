California Governor Gavin Newsom signed Senate Bill 928, which makes clear that faculty members in the California State University (CSU) system must be people, and that the instructor of record for a course must be a person who is qualified to serve as a faculty member. The law applies across all 22 CSU campuses and draws a clear distinction between using AI as a tool in teaching and giving an AI system the formal role of a faculty member or instructor of record.
