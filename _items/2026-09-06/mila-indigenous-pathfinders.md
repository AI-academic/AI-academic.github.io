The AI Governance, Policy and Inclusion division at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en) published outcomes from the third cohort of Indigenous Pathfinders in AI, a seven-week training curriculum for First Nations, Inuit and Métis participants that closed with a Pitch Day on 16 July 2026. Six prototypes were developed against community-identified needs spanning language revitalization, education, data sovereignty, social services, environmental stewardship and health; two teams, LandLens and SAI Cheese, were selected for a residency at Mila. The programme's stated framing is AI "created by Indigenous peoples, for Indigenous communities and guided by Indigenous values and priorities."
