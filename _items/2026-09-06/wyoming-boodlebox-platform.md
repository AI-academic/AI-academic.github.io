The University of Wyoming named [BoodleBox](https://boodlebox.ai/) its enterprise AI platform for faculty, staff and students, providing access to more than 38 models through a single governed environment instead of standardizing on one vendor's assistant. Awareness and training run through fall 2026 with full rollout planned for spring 2027. UW Information Technology will establish the governance framework and AI literacy programming, and the announcement emphasizes "token optimization" intended to limit the volume of institutional data sent to third-party models. Vice president for information technology Amy McLaughlin framed the choice as sparing individual faculty, staff and students from navigating an increasingly complex AI landscape on their own.
