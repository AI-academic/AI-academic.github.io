Prime Minister Carney announced Digital Transformation Canada, a federal organization consolidating expertise from Shared Services Canada, the Treasury Board Secretariat, Public Services and Procurement Canada, and ESDC's Canadian Digital Service. Its stated priorities are scaling shared digital solutions to cut duplication, equipping public servants with modern secure tools, and using federal purchasing power to grow Canadian technology companies. The release positions the body as building on "AI for All," leveraging emerging AI to scale shared government solutions while strengthening digital sovereignty, and establishes a fellowship model that brings private-sector staff into government on short-term assignments.
