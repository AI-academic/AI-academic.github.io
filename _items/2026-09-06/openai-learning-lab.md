OpenAI announced the Learning Lab, a research network intended to build an evidence base on how AI affects learning by developing common measures and studying what works across different settings. Named collaborations include the University of Tartu and Stanford on new measures of learning outcomes, Stanford's Accelerator for Learning, Oxford's AIEOU community of practice, and Cornell's National Tutoring Observatory; Bocconi's work on critical thinking and ChatGPT was among the research cited at the launch. Participating researchers are offered hands-on access to OpenAI technical teams, which situates part of the emerging evidence base inside the company whose products it evaluates.
