York University's School of Continuing Studies announced a certificate program in AI Governance, designed to prepare working professionals to manage organizational risk, accountability and compliance as AI adoption spreads. The program treats AI oversight as an emerging professional function rather than a technical specialization, extending AI education past model development into governance and organizational implementation. It is one of the first Canadian continuing-studies credentials aimed squarely at the people who will be asked to sign off on institutional AI use.
