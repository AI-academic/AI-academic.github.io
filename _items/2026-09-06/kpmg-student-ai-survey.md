KPMG Canada surveyed 1,009 Canadians aged 18 and over attending or working at educational institutions between 22 July and 5 August 2026; student respondents were 47% university, 25% college, 12% post-graduate or part-time, 12% high school and 4% technical or trade. Three-quarters of students report using generative AI for schoolwork while only 53% received formal instruction on responsible use; 81% say AI improves their understanding of course material and 51% say their critical thinking has deteriorated. Educator concerns centre on academic dishonesty (70%), over-reliance impeding learning (62%) and copyright (34%), with 79% supporting explicit AI ethics instruction and 84% worried about inequitable access. Nearly a quarter of students pay for AI tools and half believe paid access confers an academic advantage.
