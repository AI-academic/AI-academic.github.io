[Cubby](https://cubby.law/) announced partnerships with more than 20 US law schools, named ones including Emory, Wake Forest, Texas, Case Western Reserve and Quinnipiac, for a product that lets faculty deploy AI teaching assistants tuned to their own course. The assistants generate practice questions in the professor's style, grade against the professor's rubric and return one-on-one feedback in the professor's voice, all subject to faculty approval before students see it; course analytics track performance by topic and by student, and faculty materials are not used for model training. Emory associate dean Kamina Pinder is quoted saying the design enhances rather than supplants student learning.
