Times Higher Education reports a University of Auckland student disciplined for wearing AI-enabled smart glasses while taking a test, with comparable cases surfacing in Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Australia and New Zealand. A controlled test at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology found AI glasses scored 92.5% on an undergraduate exam, placing in the top five of more than 100 students. Deakin's Thomas Corbin argues that most detected cases are happenstance, so reported rates substantially understate prevalence, and that banning the devices would not resolve the underlying vulnerability of the invigilated format; Auckland's Ulrich Speidel adds that once the technology arrives as contact lenses, "it's checkmate for us."
