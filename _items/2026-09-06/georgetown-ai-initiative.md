President Eduardo Peñalver announced an initiative, coordinated with the Faculty Senate, to create an institution-wide framework for decisions about both the use and the non-use of AI in teaching, research, service and administration. Georgetown Principles of AI Use, informed by the university's Jesuit values, are due by the end of the fall 2026 semester, with practical guidance following by the end of spring 2027; departments and programs are responsible for translating the principles into course and program-level practice. The message also flags that the university is exploring technology-free spaces for distraction-free scholarly work, making non-use an explicit design choice rather than a residual category.
