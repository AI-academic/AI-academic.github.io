## Notable update (US institution)

### Arizona State makes discipline-specific AI literacy courses available across every college and school

Arizona State University (ASU), in United States, announced that, beginning in fall 2026, every college and school will offer an AI literacy course or pathway that counts toward a student's major. Faculty designed the offerings for their disciplines, and instructors retain authority to set assignment-level rules for AI use. ASU also reported an assessment-design initiative that focused on its 100 highest-enrolment courses, and included more than 400 AI workshops for faculty and staff and over 45,000 users of its institutionally supported CreateAI environment. **Read here:** [Provost Q&A: What endures as AI transforms higher education](https://news.asu.edu/20260828-university-news-provost-qa-what-endures-ai-transforms-higher-education)
