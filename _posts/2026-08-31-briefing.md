## Government updates

### California requires human faculty and instructors of record at its state university system

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed Senate Bill 928, which makes clear that faculty members in the California State University (CSU) system must be people, and that the instructor of record for a course must be a person who is qualified to serve as a faculty member. The law applies across all 22 CSU campuses and draws a clear distinction between using AI as a tool in teaching and giving an AI system the formal role of a faculty member or instructor of record. **Read here:** [Governor Newsom signs legislation](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/08/27/governor-newsom-signs-legislation-8-27-2026/), **and** [California Senate announcement and summary](https://sd31.senate.ca.gov/news/cervantes-bill-prevent-california-state-university-faculty-being-replaced-ai-signed-governor)

## Other notable updates

### Randomized trial involving ChatGPT EDU product

A preregistered randomized controlled trial involving 1,053 first-year business students at Bocconi University (Italy) found that students who had access to ChatGPT Edu performed substantially better on a short business-recommendation task. By contrast, a single-session causal-reasoning exercise did not improve overall scores, although it did lead students to produce more varied solutions and to focus more explicitly on underlying mechanisms. The study is a working paper and has not yet been peer reviewed, and several of the authors are OpenAI employees or contractors. **Read here:** [Evidence from an RCT - paper](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/novices-and-llm-august-2026.pdf)

### Higher-education online-learning leaders describe a shift from AI experimentation to evidence and governance

[EDUCAUSE](https://www.educause.edu/), [Quality Matters](https://www.qualitymatters.org/), and [Encoura Eduventures Research](https://www.encoura.org/eduventures-research/) collaborated to release the 2026 Changing Landscape of Online Education (CHLOE) Conversations series, drawing on six focus groups with 30 online-learning leaders from community colleges and public and private four-year institutions. The reports look at generative AI in the broader context of online-program strategy, quality, accessibility, student support, and the use of data. **Read here:** [2026 CHLOE Conversation Report Series](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/novices-and-llm-august-2026.pdf)
