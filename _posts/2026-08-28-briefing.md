## Canadian academic institution updates (non-Ontario)

### UBC and McGill add AI-linked research capacity through national chair awards

The University of British Columbia will recruit nine Eddie Goldenberg Research Chairs of Canada and McGill University will recruit six chairs with $52 million in federal support over eight years. UBC and McGill can also recruit nine and six Canada Impact+ Emerging Leaders, respectively. The appointments form part of the first national intake of 64 chairs and 63 emerging leaders supported by more than $541 million. **Read here:** [UBC welcomes nine world-leading researchers](https://news.ubc.ca/2026/08/eddie-goldenberg-research-chairs-of-canada/); [McGill attracts six leading researchers](https://www.mcgill.ca/newsroom/channels/news/mcgill-university-attracts-six-leading-researchers-thanks-government-canadas-1-billion-program-374130); [Government of Canada announces results for Intake 1](https://www.canada.ca/en/research-chairs/news/2026/08/government-of-canada-announces-results-for-intake-1-of-the-eddie-goldenberg-research-chairs-of-canada-and-canada-impact-emerging-leaders.html)

## Updates from other, non-academic sectors

### More than 100 organizations call for an urgent surge in AI-enabled cyber defence

OpenAI published an open letter supported by more than 100 technology, cybersecurity, finance and infrastructure organizations, including Anthropic, AWS, Google, Hugging Face, IBM and Microsoft. The signatories state that AI-enabled cyberattacks will become more widespread and sophisticated in the coming months. They call on organizations to fix high-risk weaknesses, strengthen access controls and apply a higher security bar to AI-generated code; on governments to fund and coordinate defence for under-resourced essential services; and on frontier AI companies to provide responsible model access, observability, traceable agent identities, training and support. The letter sets out voluntary principles but does not specify binding commitments, budgets or delivery deadlines. **Read here:** [A call for collective action on cyber defense](https://openai.com/collective-cyberdefense/)
