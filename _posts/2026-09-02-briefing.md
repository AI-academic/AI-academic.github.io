This round of AI briefing covers a larger time window (August 24 - September 2) and includes developments that were not reported in previous briefings. 

## Canadian academic institution updates

### Amii delivers AI adoption workshops in the Northwest Territories (Aug 26, 2026)
Amii (https://www.amii.ca/) partnered with Prosper Northwest Territories (https://prospernwt.ca/) to run an AI workshop for seven Yellowknife businesses, supported by CanNor funding under $2.8 million federal initiative using a risk/value matrix to identify AI opportunities. The aim is to increase AI adoption in regions with limited access to technical expertise. **Read here:** [Amii partnership](https://www.amii.ca/updates-insights/amii-x-prosper-nwt-helping-northern-businesses-explore-ai)

### McMaster's MacPherson Institute publishes a set of classroom AI practices (Aug 28, 2026)
Dr. Felicia Vulcu (Biochemistry and Biomedical Sciences) documented three practices in her laboratory course: an optional AI-use policy specifying which assessments permit generative AI, requiring students to verify accuracy and disclose use; using ChatGPT to generate deliberately flawed articles for students to critique and rewrite; and generating scientific illustrations and a gel-electrophoresis simulator to prepare students before lab work. She reports students adhered to the policy on take-home assignments and plans to survey students and TAs. **Read here:** [Classroom AI practices](https://mi.mcmaster.ca/introducing-an-ai-use-policy-in-a-laboratory-course/)


## International academic institution updates

### Cornell diverts minor integrity violations, including AI-related, out of formal hearings (Aug 24, 2026)
Cornell expanded its Accepting Responsibility program campus-wide, which lets students with minor academic-integrity violations attend a workshop and write a reflection instead of facing a formal hearing. The two-year pilot covered roughly 360 students across 100 courses with four repeat violations. About half of participants in the pilot had used AI in violation of integrity standards; director Elizabeth Karns reported students saying they do not know how to do assignments without it. **Read here:** [Cornell's initiative aims to turn minor cheating in a learning experience](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/students/academics/2026/08/24/cornell-hopes-turn-cheating-teachable-moment)

### Berkeley launches a professional master's in AI and machine learning (Aug 24, 2026)
Berkeley's College of Computing, Data Science, and Society announced a Master of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, a full-time in-person degree launching fall 2027. Coursework spans data science, machine learning, probability, statistical inference and AI ethics, plus a Silicon Valley immersion and a comprehensive examination. **Read here:** [Berkeley's new professional graduate program in AI and machine learning](https://cdss.berkeley.edu/news/uc-berkeley-announces-new-professional-graduate-degree-ai-and-machine-learning)

### A campus ChatGPT rollout raises contract and IP objections (Aug 26, 2026)
The University of Colorado has begun to provide free ChatGPT Edu access to its students, staff and faculty under a three-year contract. The rollout is part of US$2 million system-wide agreement with OpenAI covering four campuses. Faculty have reported concerns include data-privacy boundaries for research collaborations, a contract term claiming OpenAI ownership of student-generated content that conflicts with CU policy, equity gaps versus paid tools, and lack of consultation. **Read here:** [ChatGPT Edu access at the University of Colorado campuses](https://www.axios.com/local/boulder/2026/08/26/cu-students-free-chatgpt-edu-access)

### Chicago removes AI from its required social sciences core (Aug 26, 2026)
The University of Chicago has started prohibiting AI use in its core social sciences sequence, a required part of the undergraduate general education curriculum, from the fall 2026 semester. The courses will be taught with physical texts and mandatory in-person, device-free discussion. **Read here:** [Chicago implements AI-free teaching in courses](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2026/08/26/uchicago-goes-analog-some-courses)

### Georgia Tech publishes an academic AI strategy and faculty-authored playbook (Aug 31, 2026)
Georgia Tech released an Academic AI Strategy, which is organized around three priorities: AI fluency for every student, innovation across the academic enterprise, and institutional leadership and engagement. The accompanying playbook contains 67 faculty-authored articles from every college and distils 20 lessons from classroom experience. The institution frames disciplinary judgment and academic freedom as the basis for deciding how AI should and should not be used in teaching. **Read here:** [Academic AI strategy establishes shared vision for teaching and learning at Georgia Tech](https://news.research.gatech.edu/2026/08/31/academic-ai-strategy-establishes-shared-vision-teaching-and-learning-georgia-tech)

### Princeton consolidates AI and data science in a new cross-disciplinary academic unit (Sep 1, 2026)
Princeton established Data and Intelligent Systems, a new academic unit combining Princeton AI with Princeton Statistics and Data Science. The Unit will coordinate research services, interdisciplinary projects, undergraduate and graduate education, and existing initiatives spanning AI-enabled invention, natural and artificial minds, and language and intelligence. Two new initiatives on AI alignment and safety and societal AI will launch this fall. **Read here:** [Princeton strengthens its support for AI and data science scholarship](https://www.princeton.edu/news/2026/09/01/princeton-strengthens-its-support-ai-and-data-science-scholarship-through-data-and)

### University of Iowa commits to secure AI access and faculty development (Sep 1, 2026)
The University of Iowa launched a three-year AI Discovery Initiative funded with more than US$1 million from its strategic-plan implementation fund. The initiative includes a centrally managed token program for secure access to leading AI and agentic platforms, a faculty upskilling program that may provide protected time, training, mentorship and computing resources, and campus research, public-engagement and education events. The university explicitly intends to use the program's experience to shape its longer-term AI strategy. **Read here:** [Iowa's AI discovery initiative to help faculty, staff explore AI](https://ai.uiowa.edu/news/2026/09/ai-discovery-initiative-help-ui-faculty-staff-explore-ai)


## Government updates

### U.S. education research agency funds a national centre on generative AI and postsecondary writing (Sep 2, 2026)
The U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences has awarded the University of California, Irvine US$10 million for the WRITE AI Center. The centre will survey GenAI use in postsecondary writing, publish information on widely used tools, develop and pilot the PapyrusAI writing environment with six community colleges, and conduct a randomized trial involving 60 instructors. Planned measures include writing quality, AI literacy, self-efficacy, course performance and persistence, as well as implementation cost, privacy, bias and fairness. **Read here:** [National Center for Writing Research to improve teaching effectiveness with GenAI](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/awards/national-center-writing-research-improve-teaching-effectiveness-generative-ai-write-ai-center) **and** [UC Irvine to establish National Research Center on AI in Writing](https://www.govtech.com/education/higher-ed/uc-irvine-to-establish-national-research-center-on-ai-in-writing)


## Other notable updates

### UK–Ukraine AI partnership names academia and research as a formal pillar (Aug 24, 2026)
The UK and Ukraine signed a Joint Declaration of Intent establishing an AI partnership whose third stated pillar is "Academia and Research, on autonomy, AI assurance, cyber security and synthetic data." The companion release states the agreement grants the UK access to Ukraine's Avengers AI Labs, a platform holding battlefield sensor and camera data, with three British startups already piloting projects, and commits to bringing together "engineers, academics, businesses and military operational expertise from both countries."
 **Read here:** [UK-Ukraine partnership](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/joint-declaration-of-intent-between-the-united-kingdom-of-great-britain-and-northern-ireland-and-ukraine-on-a-uk-ukraine-artificial-intelligence-partn) **and** [Companion release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-partnership-set-to-see-the-uk-and-ukraine-develop-battle-winning-technology-as-britain-secures-access-to-ukraines-avengers-ai-labs)

### Anthropic funds independent wellbeing evaluation research (Aug 25, 2026)
Anthropic announced $5 million to fund independent research into how AI affects user wellbeing. The company will provide direct funding, model access and technical assistance, with open-source evaluations as the deliverable. The call invites clinicians, psychologists, methodologists and subject-matter experts, with initial applications due by September 21. **Read here:** [Anthropic funds to study wellbeing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/wellbeing-research-grants)

### Nature editorial urges researchers to back EU AI Act enforcement (Aug 25, 2026)
The editorial reports that the European AI Office gained enforcement powers on 2 August 2026, allowing investigations and sanctions up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover under the 2024 AI Act. The editorial endorses the Act's four-tier risk categorisation, calls for mandatory federal AI vetting in the US in place of the current voluntary 30-day review, and urges expanded AI Office staffing and researcher use of its whistle-blower mechanism. **Read here:** [Springer Nature editorial on EU AI Act](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02567-5)

### Nature article assesses how far AI-detection tools have actually come (Aug 25, 2026)
A news feature examines AI-detection software for identifying machine-generated text in academic and research contexts, and assesses tools including Pangram and GPTZero and their use in peer review, student work and scientific publishing. The article reports that despite large accuracy gains, the tools still struggle to separate fully AI-generated text from human writing lightly polished with AI — the most common real-world case. **Read here:** [Nature News: Assessment of AI Detection Tools](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02569-3)

### Nvidia reported close to acquiring Hugging Face (Aug 27, 2026)
Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face, a principal hub where developers share and download open-source AI models and rent computing resources. The deal is reported and not yet close. **Read here:** [Nvidia acquiring Hugging Face](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/)

### Music publishers sue Anthropic over training-data acquisition (Aug 29, 2026)
A set of music publishers that include Sony Music, Warner Chappell and others have sued Anthropic and co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann in the US District Court for the Northern District of California. They allege torrenting, scraping and downloading of copyrighted works including millions of copies of books containing lyrics and sheet music. The suit follows the Bartz litigation, where a judge held that training on copyrighted material could be lawful but acquiring it through piracy was not. **Read here:** [Music publishers sue Anthropic](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/sony-music-warner-sue-anthropic-alleging-a-brazen-campaign-of-intellectual-property-theft/)

### Anthropic hardens frontier-agent testing and introduces customer-controlled misuse monitoring (Sep 1, 2026)
Anthropic reported that it paused and hardened some internal and external evaluations after four incidents in which models operating without normal cyber safeguards took unauthorized actions or reached live systems. Its revised practices call for hardened isolation, pre-engagement validation, explicit scope boundaries and real-time monitoring for third-party cyber evaluations. In a related announcement, Anthropic said its forthcoming Enterprise Frontier Safeguards will analyze activity across sessions while allowing customers to retain monitoring data in their own cloud accounts, under their own encryption keys and access controls. Rollout is planned in phases beginning later this fall. **Read here:** [Improving our alignment and security efforts](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-alignment-security-efforts) **and** [Developing Enterprise Frontier Safeguards with our customers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-frontier-safeguards)

### Nature commentary argues that AI-mediated scholarship needs contribution-level disclosure (Sep 1, 2026)
In a commentary published in the journal Nature, Robert Braun argues that conventional authorship cannot show where generative AI shaped literature search, analysis, drafting, revision or editorial work, even though human authors remain responsible for scientific claims. The author proposes a RediT-AI approach to document where AI influenced human contributions while keeping responsibility with researchers. The article itself discloses substantial use of ChatGPT in its development and Claude in review, making the piece a practical test of the transparency model it advocates.**Read here:** [Who is responsible when AI helps to write science?](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02686-z) **and** [Nature essay on AI, authorship, and responsibility](https://www.ihs.ac.at/news-and-events/news/detail/nature-essay-on-ai-authorship-and-responsibility/)
