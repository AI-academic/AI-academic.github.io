This site collects news on how artificial intelligence is affecting universities, colleges, and academic research, in Canada and internationally. Each issue, published roughly once a week, covers developments at academic institutions, government policy, funding, and industry and journal news.

I write and edit this site, and I use AI tools at several stages of the work. Candidate stories come from a weekly search run with multiple AI agents, as well as my own reading and web searches. The AI tools also draft the short summaries and help format them for the site. Before publishing, the briefings are manually screened for relevance, content and source accuracy, and adjusted to appropriate length. 

I take full ownership of the work. If you find an error, please [email me](mailto:bgupta.bio@gmail.com?subject=AI-Academic%20correction) and I will correct it.
