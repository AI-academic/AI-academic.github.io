This site collects news on how artificial intelligence is affecting universities, colleges and academic research, in Canada and internationally. Each issue, published about once a week, covers developments at academic institutions, government policy and funding, and industry and journal news that bears on teaching, research and how institutions are run.

## How these briefings are made

I write and edit this site, and I use AI tools at several stages of the work. I want readers to know exactly where.

**Finding stories.** Candidate stories come from three places: a weekly search run with Claude (from Anthropic), searches with ChatGPT (from OpenAI), and my own reading and web searches.

**Drafting.** The AI tools also draft the short summaries and help format them for the site.

**Checking and editing.** I decide which stories run. Before anything is published I check each item against its original sources, including dates, figures and quotes, and I rewrite, shorten or drop items as needed. AI output is treated as a lead to verify, never as a source, and every item links to the reporting or documents it rests on. Where an item closes with a comment on what the news means, that comment is mine.

**Responsibility.** I take full responsibility for everything published here. If you find an error, please [email me](mailto:bgupta.bio@gmail.com?subject=AI-Academic%20correction) and I will correct it.

The site sometimes reports on Anthropic and OpenAI. Using their tools has no bearing on which of their stories are covered or how.
