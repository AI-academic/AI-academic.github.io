This site collects news on how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is affecting universities, colleges, and academic research, in Canada and internationally. Each issue, published typically once a week, covers developments at academic institutions, government policy, funding, and industry and journal news.

I write and edit this site, and I use AI tools at several stages of the work. Candidate stories come from a weekly search that involves multiple AI agents and from my own reading and web searches. The AI tools also draft the short summaries and help format them for the site. The briefings are then edited by hand before publishing. This involves deciding which stories to publish, carefully screening them for relevance, content, and source accuracy, deleting unnecessary information, and manually adjusting their length. I also check all links to original stories/reports and fix when necessary. 

I take full ownership of the work. If you find an error, please [email me](mailto:bgupta.bio@gmail.com?subject=AI-Academic%20correction) and I will correct it.
